Librarian calculator

The books librarians sell are not up to each villager. The game draws every librarian's trades from one list per villager level, set by the world seed and shared by the whole world: the 10th time any librarian gets its first trades, it gets the 10th entry of the list. Breaking and placing the lectern again just moves on to the next entry, so once you know where your world is in the list, you know how many times to reroll for the book you want.

Your world

In single player /seed works even without cheats; on a server, ask an operator. A villager's first trades are level 1 (novice).

1. Find where your world is

Reroll your librarian a few times: break the lectern, place it back and open the trade screen. Write down each screen in order: its enchanted book, the book's level and its price in emeralds, or "no book". Two or three screens with a book are usually enough (a screen without one tells less). In a new world, the first librarian that ever gets its trades shows entry 0.

2. How many rerolls to the book you want

How it works

Every list of villager trades in the game names its own random sequence (a librarian's first trades: minecraft:trade_set/librarian/level_1). The game keeps one random generator per sequence for the whole world, seeded from the world seed and the sequence's name, and draws those trades from it; the villager's own random numbers never come into it. So the calculator lists the entries from the world seed alone.

Any librarian that gets new trades uses up an entry: a librarian working at its lectern gets its trades even if nobody looks, and so do the villagers of a village nearby. Open the trade screen after every reroll so each one counts once, and if a screen stops matching, find your place again from two screens. Prices change with your reputation (curing a zombie villager or the Hero of the Village effect lowers them): if yours differ, leave the price empty.

Checked against the real 26.3 game: 300 rerolls in a row in two worlds and a villager rerolling by itself with a lectern, every trade, book and price the same.
