Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

Every time the input changes (turns on or turns off), the output gives one short pulse of L = 16 ticks (0.8 s), 4 ticks (0.2 s) after the change. It handles every input pulse of 30 ticks (1.5 s) or longer.

Use it to react to any change of a lever, in both directions.

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the demo on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same sequence is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: press and hold the IN button of the bar (or the cell tagged IN) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2-tick pulse.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged OUT: the orange glass marker next to it glows while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Inputs and output over time

The input follows this schedule (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 ticks = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole sequence lasts 969 ticks (48.45 s).

Notes from its designer

A 16-tick pulse every time the input turns on and every time it turns off, for inputs of 1.5 seconds or more, 13 blocks at any length (8 ticks per item in its first hopper). A copper bulb in the input cell changes state on both edges, an observer watching it toggles a second copper bulb, and while that bulb is lit its comparator locks the second hopper and unlocks the first, so the items pass one by one; when the first hopper is empty, its comparator goes off and a torch toggles the bulb off; then the items flow back, ready for the next edge. This one holds 2 items: 16 ticks. Detail: an edge that comes while the items are still moving toggles the bulb early and cuts that pulse short (min-pulse about 2 L: the run plus the items' way back).
