Adder slice: full adders that stack into an adder of any width (ripple-carry adder)

adder slice: copies stack into any width · 54 blocks

A full adder made to be copied: stack copies side by side and each copy's carry out feeds the next copy's carry in, so N copies add two N-bit numbers (a ripple-carry adder).

passed at every position and facing we tested · 54 blocks per bit · reacts in 18 ticks (0.9 s), 12 ticks (0.6 s) more for each bit after the first · fits in 8×2×8

Try it

Turn on A0, B0 and CIN (the carry in) with the buttons under the view: S0 lights when one or three are on, COUT when two or more are (1 + 1 + 1 = 11 in binary). To add longer numbers, stack copies side by side: each copy's COUT is the next copy's CIN. Press play under the view to watch our test on a loop; touch any other control to take over.

How this design does it: Each bit is two half adders side by side: each half adder's carry feeds a repeater, the two lines join, and a last repeater lights a lamp that is the next bit's carry-in.

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: press and hold the A0 button of the bar (or the cell tagged A0) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse; press and hold the B0 button of the bar (or the cell tagged B0) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse; press and hold the CIN button of the bar (or the cell tagged CIN) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged COUT, S0: the orange glass marker next to it glows while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Our test in the real game

The judge drives the inputs with this schedule and checks the output after every change; here it is simulated (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 gt = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole test lasts 1289 ticks (1 min 4.45 s).

Notes from its designer

Each bit is two half adders side by side: each half adder's carry feeds a repeater, the two lines join, and a last repeater lights a lamp that is the next bit's carry-in. Copies stacked every 7 blocks make a ripple-carry adder of any width (judged at 1 to 4 and 8 bits).
