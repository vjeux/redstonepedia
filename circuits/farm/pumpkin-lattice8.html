Pumpkin farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

664 pumpkins an hour · 64 stems · 800 blocks · 0.83 per block · ~2,200 iron · needs open sky or light 9

Harvests pumpkins by itself, forever, from its 64 stems: every pumpkin that grows beside a stem is broken and it goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 664 pumpkins an hour: 0.83 for each block placed.

passed at every position and facing we tested · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · fits in 17×6×17

664 pumpkins an hourCrop farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

After building it, plant a pumpkin seed on each of its 64 farmland blocks and put a chest under the end of its collector hopper line (it gathers about 0.2 pumpkins a second; one hopper line carries 2.5): the download has neither (our test adds them, and they do not count in its blocks). It needs open sky, or light 9 at every stem (a torch or lantern beside it): in the dark, indoors or underground, nothing grows; with light 9 it works day and night. Building it by hand, put each hopper minecart in with the rail trick: place a rail where the cart goes, put the cart on it, then break the rail.

Try it

The view plants the pumpkins and puts our test's chest under its hopper, then runs it like our test, the pumpkins growing about 12 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds). To farm more, build copies side by side, one every 17 blocks.

How this design does it: 64 pumpkin stems two blocks apart on wet farmland, with a fruit cell on mud between every two stems (each stem has four, each shared with a neighbour) and wet farmland on every diagonal, so every stem grows at the fastest rate (a pumpkin every 4 tries).

How to try it

The farm runs in the view the way our test does: the view plants the crop and puts our test's chests at its outputs, then grows it about 12 times faster than the game; every pumpkin that grows beside a stem is broken and it drops into the chest at OUT. The numbers over each chest show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

64 pumpkin stems two blocks apart on wet farmland, with a fruit cell on mud between every two stems (each stem has four, each shared with a neighbour) and wet farmland on every diagonal, so every stem grows at the fastest rate (a pumpkin every 4 tries). An observer over each stem fires the four pistons over its fruit cells; the squeezed pumpkin drops into a hopper minecart under the mud, and hopper lines under the rows carry it to one collector line and one chest. Needs open sky or light 9 at the stems. The carts go in with the rail trick (rail, cart, break the rail). 2187 iron. Copies side by side every 17 blocks.

name: pumpkin-lattice8
# A full-size pumpkin farm: 8 x 8 stems two blocks apart on wet farmland, wet farmland on every
# diagonal and a fruit cell (on mud) on every side of every stem but the rim's, each shared by the two
# stems beside it (a fruit that grows in an observer's 2 gt blind spot is broken by the other stem's
# next harvest). Wet farmland around makes a stem roll 1 in 4 (CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed), and every
# roll grows a pumpkin on a free side. An observer above each stem fires when it attaches; the dust on
# its top powers the cells above the four pistons around it, which push down into the fruit cells.
# The squeezed drop goes down through the mud into a hopper minecart, which a hopper empties into a
# chest. Waterlogged grates on diagonals keep the farmland wet. Written by `circuit farm-gen`.
