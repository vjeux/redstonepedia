Bamboo farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

fuels ~280 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 12 h, a tenth of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed · 64 bamboo stalks · 858 blocks · 389 iron

Harvests bamboo by itself, forever, from its 64 stalks: every block that grows is broken and its item goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 1,100 bamboo an hour: 1.3 for each block placed. As furnace fuel, its bamboo smelts about 280 items an hour: a double chest of ore every 12 h, a tenth of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed.

the obvious design, the bar the pick of its crop is measured against: no flying machine, no slime blocks · 0.33 items smelted an hour per block · needs open sky or light 9 · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · harvests each stalk at 3 blocks tall · fits in 66×6×4

After building it, plant a bamboo on each of its 64 mud blocks and run its collector hopper line into your super smelter's fuel chest: the download has no bamboo (our test adds it, and a chest at the end of its line, which do not count in its blocks). It needs open sky, or light 9 at every stalk (a torch or lantern beside it): in the dark, indoors or underground, nothing grows; with light 9 it works day and night.

Try it

The view plants the bamboo and puts our test's chest under its hopper, then runs it like our test, the bamboo growing 8 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds).

How this design does it: The obvious bamboo fuel farm, 64 stalks in a row: each stalk on mud over one hopper line into a chest, a piston beside its 2nd block and an observer above the piston watching its 3rd; the observers' dust behind them is one line, so any stalk reaching 3 fires every piston.

Notes from its designer

The obvious bamboo fuel farm, 64 stalks in a row: each stalk on mud over one hopper line into a chest, a piston beside its 2nd block and an observer above the piston watching its 3rd; the observers' dust behind them is one line, so any stalk reaching 3 fires every piston. The fuel farm's bar: 280 items smelted per hour (bamboo burns 50 gt), 0.326 per block. Its walls are what make it work: a piston squeezes each popped item against what is in front of it and the item shoots up and out, so glass walls in front, at both ends and over the stalks and observers keep every item in (glass lets the light through: bamboo grows only at light 9), and a stone row under the front glass closes the gap an item pushed down along the wall slipped out of.
