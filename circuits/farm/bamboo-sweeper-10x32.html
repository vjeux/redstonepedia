Bamboo farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

fuels ~1,350 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 2.5 h, half of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed · 319 bamboo stalks · 1,468 blocks · ~2,500 iron + 92 slime

Harvests bamboo by itself, forever, from its 319 stalks: every block that grows is broken and its item goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 5,400 bamboo an hour: 3.7 for each block placed. As furnace fuel, its bamboo smelts about 1,350 items an hour: a double chest of ore every 2.5 h, half of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed.

half the field of the pick of its crop (319 stalks against 639) · 0.93 items smelted an hour per block · one flying machine sweeps the field · needs open sky or light 9 · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · harvests each stalk at 7 blocks tall · fits in 14×7×42

fuels ~1,350 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 2.5 h, half of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speedCrop farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

After building it, plant a bamboo on each of its 319 mud blocks and run its two output hopper lines into your super smelter's fuel chest: the download has no bamboo (our test adds it, and a chest at the end of each line, which do not count in its blocks). It needs open sky, or light 9 at every stalk (a torch or lantern beside it): in the dark, indoors or underground, nothing grows; with light 9 it works day and night.

Try it

The view plants the bamboo and puts our test's chests under its hoppers, then runs it like our test, the bamboo growing 3 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds). To farm more, build copies side by side, one every 15 blocks.

How this design does it: Bamboo fuel farm swept by one flying machine, 319 stalks on a 10x32 field: half the fuel an 8-furnace super smelter burns at full speed.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

harvests its crop by itself: every block that grows is broken and its item sent into a chest, forever, with no replanting

How to try it

The farm runs in the view the way our test does: the view plants the crop and puts our test's chests at its outputs, then grows it 3 times faster than the game; every block that grows is broken and its item drops into the 2 chests at OUT. The numbers over each chest show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Bamboo fuel farm swept by one flying machine, 319 stalks on a 10x32 field: half the fuel an 8-furnace super smelter burns at full speed. The same design as bamboo-sweeper-8x8 at the widest field one machine can sweep (10: each push moves the half's slime, observer, a 4-column wing of slime over stone blades and the other half's piston, 11 of the 12 blocks a piston can move), longer: mud over a hopper under every stalk, the 6-block two-way flying machine whose stone blades cut every stalk at its 2nd block (bamboo keeps no growth memory, so a timed cut loses nothing), walls up to y 4 along the sides and y 5 at the ends, catcher rows at both ends, and a catcher behind each end wall (an item riding on top of the machine is squeezed into the end wall as it docks, and the game moves an item stuck in a block to the closest free space, which can be behind the wall). 1357 items smelted per hour (bamboo burns 50 gt): a double chest of ore every 2.5 h.
