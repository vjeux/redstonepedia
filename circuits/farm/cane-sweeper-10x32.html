Sugar cane farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

800 sugar cane an hour · 256 canes · 1,091 blocks · 0.73 per block · ~2,200 iron + 92 slime · one flying machine sweeps the field

Harvests sugar cane by itself, forever, from its 256 canes: every block that grows is broken and its item goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 800 sugar cane an hour: 0.73 for each block placed.

the biggest harvest per block, of 3 tested · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · harvests each cane at 2 blocks tall · fits in 14×6×42

After building it, plant a sugar cane on each of its 256 mud blocks and put a chest under the end of each of its 2 hopper lines: the download has neither (our test adds them, and they do not count in its blocks). Needs water where the view shows it.

Try it

The view plants the sugar cane and puts our test's chests under its hoppers, then runs it like our test, the sugar cane growing 10 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds). To farm more, build copies side by side, one every 15 blocks.

How this design does it: Full-size sugar cane farm swept by one flying machine, 256 canes on a 10x32 field: the same water lattice, hopper floor and ring as the piston farm, but no piston per cane.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

harvests its crop by itself: every block that grows is broken and its item sent into a chest, forever, with no replanting

How to try it

The farm runs in the view the way our test does: the view plants the crop and puts our test's chests at its outputs, then grows it 10 times faster than the game; every block that grows is broken and its item drops into the 2 chests at OUT. The numbers over each chest show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Full-size sugar cane farm swept by one flying machine, 256 canes on a 10x32 field: the same water lattice, hopper floor and ring as the piston farm, but no piston per cane. A 6-block two-way flying machine (two halves of observer, slime and sticky piston that push and pull each other one block every 10 ticks) runs the length of the field at the canes' 2nd-block height with a wing on each half (slime with a stone blade under it, 4 columns a side) and destroys every 2nd block it passes. A station at each end (an observer that sees it arrive, the mud it powers, dust behind the leading piston) sends it back, so it shuttles for ever, 678 ticks a round trip; it starts when the cane under its first observer is planted. Its slime launches the items it touches along the track, so walls past both ends, a curb along the sides, catcher rows, a pocket behind each station's dust and a hopper under its mud bring every item to the hoppers; two collectors, two chests. The machine and its ends cost the same at any length, so this longer field spends fewer blocks per cane than the 10x24. 95% of the plant ceiling at vanilla speed.
