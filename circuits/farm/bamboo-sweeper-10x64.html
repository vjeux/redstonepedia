Bamboo farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

fuels ~2,750 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 1.3 h, 95% of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed · 639 bamboo stalks · 2,492 blocks · ~4,400 iron + 92 slime

Harvests bamboo by itself, forever, from its 639 stalks: every block that grows is broken and its item goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 11,000 bamboo an hour: 4.4 for each block placed. As furnace fuel, its bamboo smelts about 2,750 items an hour: a double chest of ore every 1.3 h, 95% of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed.

the biggest harvest per block, of 3 tested · 1.1 items smelted an hour per block · one flying machine sweeps the field · needs open sky or light 9 · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · harvests each stalk at 7 blocks tall · fits in 14×7×74

fuels ~2,750 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 1.3 h, 95% of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speedCrop farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

After building it, plant a bamboo on each of its 639 mud blocks and run its two output hopper lines into your super smelter's fuel chest: the download has no bamboo (our test adds it, and a chest at the end of each line, which do not count in its blocks). It needs open sky, or light 9 at every stalk (a torch or lantern beside it): in the dark, indoors or underground, nothing grows; with light 9 it works day and night.

Try it

The view plants the bamboo and puts our test's chests under its hoppers, then runs it like our test, the bamboo growing 1.3 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds). To farm more, build copies side by side, one every 15 blocks.

How this design does it: Bamboo fuel farm swept by one flying machine, 639 stalks on a 10x64 field: one field keeps an 8-furnace super smelter at 95% of full speed.

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. Verified in the real game. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Blocks used: hopper mud observer obsidian redstone wire slime block sticky piston stone

Which to build: this farm fuels more for each block placed (1.1 items smelted an hour against 0.33, 3.4 times as much), but it costs 92 slime and a flying machine that travels its field. The obvious fuel farm, the bar it is measured against, has no flying machine and no slime blocks: Crop farm: fuels ~280 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 12 h, a tenth of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed · 64 bamboo stalks · 858 blocks · 389 iron.

For a super smelter: run its lines into the fuel chest of Auto-smelter: a double chest of raw iron in 72 min (100% of 8 furnaces) · ~8 stacks of coal · 108 blocks · rests.

Half the field: ~1,350 items smelted an hour · 1,468 blocks.

In depth

harvests its crop by itself: every block that grows is broken and its item sent into a chest, forever, with no replanting

How to try it

The farm runs in the view the way our test does: the view plants the crop and puts our test's chests at its outputs, then grows it 1.3 times faster than the game; every block that grows is broken and its item drops into the 2 chests at OUT. The numbers over each chest show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Bamboo fuel farm swept by one flying machine, 639 stalks on a 10x64 field: one field keeps an 8-furnace super smelter at 95% of full speed. The same design as bamboo-sweeper-8x8 at the widest field one machine can sweep (10: each push moves the half's slime, observer, a 4-column wing of slime over stone blades and the other half's piston, 11 of the 12 blocks a piston can move), longer: mud over a hopper under every stalk, the 6-block two-way flying machine whose stone blades cut every stalk at its 2nd block (bamboo keeps no growth memory, so a timed cut loses nothing), walls up to y 4 along the sides and y 5 at the ends, catcher rows at both ends, and a catcher behind each end wall (an item riding on top of the machine is squeezed into the end wall as it docks, and the game moves an item stuck in a block to the closest free space, which can be behind the wall). 2,750 items smelted per hour (bamboo burns 50 gt): a double chest of ore every 1.26 h; a 10x67 field reaches full speed.
