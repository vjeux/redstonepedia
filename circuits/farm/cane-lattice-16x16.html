Sugar cane farm: harvests by itself (automatic farm)

646 sugar cane an hour · 204 canes · 1,234 blocks · 0.52 per block · ~1,800 iron · needs nothing but water

Harvests sugar cane by itself, forever, from its 204 canes: every block that grows is broken and its item goes into a chest. At the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) it gathers about 646 sugar cane an hour: 0.52 for each block placed.

no flying machine, no slime blocks (the pick of its crop sweeps its field with one) · at the game's normal growth speed (random tick speed 3) · harvests each cane at 2 blocks tall · fits in 20×7×25

646 sugar cane an hourCrop farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

After building it, plant a sugar cane on each of its 204 mud blocks and put a chest under the end of each of its 2 hopper lines: the download has neither (our test adds them, and they do not count in its blocks). Comes loaded with 38 stone in its containers: the download includes them; building it by hand, put them in first. Needs water where the view shows it.

Try it

The view plants the sugar cane and puts our test's chests under its hoppers, then runs it like our test, the sugar cane growing 10 times faster than in the game: watch the farm break each block that grows and send its item into the chest (the tag over each chest shows what it holds). To farm more, build copies side by side, one every 21 blocks.

How this design does it: Full-size sugar cane farm, 204 canes on a 16x16 field: water on every fifth cell of a lattice (x + 2z = 0 mod 5) gives every cane exactly one water neighbour; mud under the canes and a hopper under every cell and under a one-cell ring around the field (the lattice continues into it, so edge canes keep their water) catch every item.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

harvests its crop by itself: every block that grows is broken and its item sent into a chest, forever, with no replanting

How to try it

The farm runs in the view the way our test does: the view plants the crop and puts our test's chests at its outputs, then grows it 10 times faster than the game; every block that grows is broken and its item drops into the 2 chests at OUT. The numbers over each chest show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Full-size sugar cane farm, 204 canes on a 16x16 field: water on every fifth cell of a lattice (x + 2z = 0 mod 5) gives every cane exactly one water neighbour; mud under the canes and a hopper under every cell and under a one-cell ring around the field (the lattice continues into it, so edge canes keep their water) catch every item. A downward piston above each cane breaks its 2nd block; all 204 fire at once through a carpet of dust on the piston tops, fed from both sides by two repeater lines, on each pulse of one 30 s hopper clock (hopclk-sealed-p600, inverted by a torch into a short pulse). The hopper rows carry the items to two collector columns, each into its own chest. 96% of the plant ceiling at vanilla speed.
