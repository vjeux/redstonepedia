Try it

Tap IN under the view to turn the input on, again to turn it off: turn it on, then off: OUT stays red a while after (the box marked OUT turns red while the output is on). Press play under the view to watch our test on a loop; touch any other control to take over.

How this design does it: Keeps its output on for about 50 seconds (990 ticks) after the input turns off, in 8 blocks: two hoppers pass 125 cobblestone back and forth, and a comparator keeps the output on while the first hopper still holds any; it needs an input about as long as its delay to load the items back (one per 8 ticks), and no hopper top a stray item can reach.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

The output turns on with the input (4 gt (0.2 s) later) and stays on 990 gt (49.5 s) after the input turns off: every output pulse is the input's length plus 990 gt (49.5 s). Input pulses closer together than that merge into one long output pulse (a light or a door that stays on a while after the last press of a pressure plate).

It reacts to input pulses of 1280 gt (1 min 4 s) or longer.

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop: input pulses of 1 to 80 gt (each output pulse lasts 990 gt (49.5 s) longer than its input), then groups of 3 short pulses with growing gaps (closer than L, they merge into one output pulse). The badge reads demo with the step it is on, and the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below. Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: tap the IN button of the bar (or the cell tagged IN) to turn that input on or off (it powers the cell with a redstone block): turn the input on, then off: the output stays on 990 gt (49.5 s) after you turn it off.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged OUT: the redstone lamp in that cell (the lamp the judge itself adds to read the output) lights while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Our test in the real game

The judge drives the input with this schedule and checks the output after every change; here it is simulated (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 gt = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole test lasts 63841 ticks (53 min 12.05 s).
