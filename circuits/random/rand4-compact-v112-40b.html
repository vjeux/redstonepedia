Try it

Press IN under the view, holding it a moment like a button press: one of the outputs (A, B, C or D) lights and stays lit until the next press picks again, at random. The view first plays our test on a loop; touch any control to take over.

How this design does it: Two coin flips do the picking: rand4-compact's mechanism rearranged by clk-rt's circuit compact, judged box 112 (was 140), 40 blocks (was 43); its blocks sit in 7x4x4.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

each press lights exactly one of its outputs, picked at random, every output as often as the others

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: press and hold the IN button of the bar (or the cell tagged IN) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged A, B, C, D: the orange glass marker next to it glows while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Our test in the real game

The judge drives the input with this schedule and checks the output after every change; here it is simulated (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 gt = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole test lasts 5081 ticks (4 min 14.05 s).
