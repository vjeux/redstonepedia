Automatic door: opens as you walk up, closes behind you (2×2 piston door)

opens as you walk up, closes behind you · 91 blocks · you wait up to 0.65 s at the door

A 2×2 piston door that opens by itself when you walk up from either side and closes behind you, even when two people walk through close together. It also stays open for someone standing in the doorway, but if someone else walks through while you stand there, it can still close on you and hurt you (half a heart per hurt) until you step out.

A mob on a plate opens it too: nothing in vanilla tells a player from a mob, so put the plates where mobs can't walk. Its outer rows are tripwire strings, which a dropped item presses too: keep items off them.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A 2×2 piston door: four sticky pistons pull the four door blocks into the wall. Its detectors lie in the walkway on both sides of the door, where you step anyway: stone pressure plates, which feel players and mobs but never dropped items (a wooden plate would feel items too), or tripwire strings, which feel anything that crosses them. A plate powers the block under it, and the signal runs under the floor to the redstone that holds the pistons closed: step on a plate and the door opens.

A stone plate stays down for at least 1 second after it is pressed and checks again every second, letting go once nobody is on it, so the door closes a second or two after the last person has passed. The doorway itself is only one block deep: you cross it, between the plates on either side, in a fraction of a second.

Nothing can feel someone standing in the doorway: no detector fits there, since the pistons sweep it and a plate or a string pushed into pops off. A door with no memory, the simplest, closes on them; one with memory reads the order its plates were pressed in and stays open for them, for about twice the blocks.

Notes from its designer

The fewest blocks: lock-v10 with the two tripwire rows joined by one dust line beside the door at the walkway's floor level. It runs right under the right hooks' blocks, which power the dust below them, so no wiring under the floor and no blocks to stop dust climbing where it must not.
