How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: press and hold the A0 button of the bar (or the cell tagged A0) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse; press and hold the A1 button of the bar (or the cell tagged A1) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged Q0, Q1, Q2, Q3: the redstone lamp in that cell (the lamp the judge itself adds to read the output) lights while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Our test in the real game

Notes from its designer

A torch hung on each input gives its opposite. One wire touches both torches: it carries "a0 or a1 is off", at full strength beside a lit torch and one level weaker a block away. Comparators in subtract mode each read an input or an opposite at the back and a wire at a side, and an output stays lit only while its comparator's side is weaker than its back, so exactly one of the four outputs is lit for each pair of inputs.
