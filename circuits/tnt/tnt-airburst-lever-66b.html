Try it

Flip its lever (the IN button under the view) to start it: watch a lit TNT come out of the machine each cycle while its TNT block stays in place; flip it back and it stops. The view first plays our test on a loop; touch any control to take over.

How this design does it: Precise air burst with an on/off lever, ONE clock + ONE item-free delay, in the 56b's box (5x5x11): the 56b's dropper stays, EMPTY (the engine's obsidian still fires it once per TNT, and its `triggered` state starts the delay); an observer watching it starts 56 gt of repeaters laid where the hopper ring was (y = 0 under the clock and up x = 7: out of reach of the bursts and of the frame's slime), ending in the repeater whose observer pulses the launcher's obsidian.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

It keeps lighting TNT forever without using up its TNT block: about 22 TNT a minute. Every 54 gt (2.7 s) a piston pushes the TNT block: a piston copies every block it moves before it clears the old cells, and a TNT that gets a block update while powered at that instant is lit where it stands (a primed TNT that falls and explodes 80 gt, 4 s, later) while its moved copy lands as a TNT block again. Each push lights 1 TNT.

How to try it

The duper runs by itself as soon as it is built: the view plays it from the start. Watch the piston push the TNT block every cycle and a lit TNT (flashing white) drop out of the machine each time, while the TNT block lands again in its place; a lit TNT vanishes when its fuse runs out instead of exploding. Pause and step one game tick at a time with the bar's buttons; drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Our test in the real game

The judge drives the input with this schedule and checks the output after every change; here it is simulated (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 gt = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole test lasts 2507 ticks (2 min 5.35 s).
