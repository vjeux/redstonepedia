Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

It keeps lighting TNT forever without using up its TNT block: about 86 TNT a minute. Every 0.7 s a piston pushes the TNT block: a piston copies every block it moves before it clears the old cells, and a TNT that gets a block update while powered at that instant is lit where it stands (a primed TNT that falls and explodes 4 s, 4 s, later) while its moved copy lands as a TNT block again. Each push lights 1 TNT.

How to try it

The duper runs by itself as soon as it is built: press play under the view to watch it. Watch the piston push the TNT block every cycle and a lit TNT (flashing white) drop out of the machine each time, while the TNT block lands again in its place; a lit TNT vanishes when its fuse runs out instead of exploding. Pause and step one game tick at a time with the bar's buttons; drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

The TNT duper: 86 lit TNT a minute, standing right on the ground. Two catalog parts. The 16-block duplicator engine (tnt-triggered-launch-16b's, without its input block) makes one TNT each time its sticky piston loses power for a moment: held extended, released, it retracts, the redstone block on its slime frame comes back next to its dust and pushes it out again, which primes a TNT and throws it north at 1 block per tick. Each TNT lands and explodes on the ground 9 to 30 blocks away (7.4 to 27 on unbreakable ground), beyond the 6.9 blocks a blast can reach, so no explosion touches the machine and each one breaks ground blocks. obsidian at 2 -1 2 stays for immovability (pistons must not move it: the design fails or changes even with a blast-proof netherite block there).
