Try it

The view shows the elevator as built, without items. In the game, start its clock by placing its last block where the view puts its start block, then fill the bottom chest: every item rises into the top chest.

How this design does it: A dropper elevator 5 blocks up (14 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of droppers facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest.

Notes from its designer

A dropper elevator 5 blocks up (14 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of droppers facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest. One observer clock fires the column: its observer at the second dropper fires droppers 0-2 and leads (its partner is placed last: a player starts the clock by placing it). A dropper also reads the power of the dropper two above (quasi-connectivity), so each group must be off whenever the group below turns off.
