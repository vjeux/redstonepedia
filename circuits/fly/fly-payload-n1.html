Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A flying machine: pistons, slime or honey and observers that push each other along. Once started (the start block is placed), it moves itself one block at a time and keeps going until something stops it.

It moves one block every 10 gt (2.0 blocks per second).

How to try it

The machine starts by itself (its start block is placed when the view begins): press play and watch it fly along its corridor; reset to see it again. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
