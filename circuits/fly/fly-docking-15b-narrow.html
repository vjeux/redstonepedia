Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A two-way flying machine: pistons, slime or honey and observers that push each other along, then come back. Once started it shuttles back and forth by itself.

How to try it

The machine starts by itself (its start block is placed when the view begins): press play and watch it shuttle between its docking stations. Flip its lever with the IN button under the view: off, it docks at the lever's station and stays; on, it leaves again. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Flying machine with docking stations, 15 blocks, and an on/off lever: a 6-block flying machine (two sticky pistons, two slime blocks, two observers) shuttles between two docking stations, 98 ticks per round trip. Each station has a wall that stops the machine and an observer that sees it arrive and fires into a dust behind the leading piston, which pushes the machine back the other way. At the station with the lever, the observer stands on a sticky piston: lever off pulls it aside, so the machine docks there and stays; lever on pushes it back into place, and a moved observer fires, so the docked machine leaves at once. It survives the lever flipped back too soon. fly-docking-15b with its lever station tucked in (4 wide instead of 5): `circuit compact` on the full docking judge (four on/off cycles, the real-use flips and reflips, the ground), track fly 2026-10-21. material: 1 obsidian -> stone (the same kind, axis and metrics on its judge); obsidian at 1 0 6 stays for immovability (pistons must not move it: the design fails or changes even with a blast-proof netherite block there)
