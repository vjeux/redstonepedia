Auto-brewer

1 brewing stand · 42 blocks

Brews the water bottles of its bottles chest into potions of swiftness in its brewing stand: it puts in one nether wart (awkward potions), then one sugar, burns blaze powder from its fuel chest and collects every finished potion in its output chest, 1.9 potions a minute per stand.

Our test brews potions of swiftness (nether wart, then sugar); another second ingredient in the sugar chest (a magma cream, a golden carrot...) brews its potion the same way. Keep the chests in order: sugar on water bottles makes useless mundane potions.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

brews the water bottles of its bottles chest into potions of swiftness, a nether wart first and a sugar after it, burns blaze powder from its fuel chest and collects every finished potion in its output chest

How to try it

The brewer runs in the view the way our test does: water bottles start in the chest tagged BOTTLES, blaze powder in FUEL, nether wart in WART and sugar in SUGAR; the machine puts one wart, then one sugar, into each brewing stand for every three bottles, and every finished potion drops into the chest at OUT. The numbers over each container show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button (a brew takes 20 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

One brewing stand in a 3x6x5 box: a clock (two hoppers passing 106 stones back and forth, a copper bulb remembering the direction) splits time into two halves of 42 seconds. When the brewing half starts, the clock's own torch flashes under the block next to the bulb, which fires two droppers beside it: one lifts a nether wart into the hoppers over the stand, the other a sugar that takes two more hoppers and so arrives later, then waits until the wart is used up: the stand brews awkward potions, then swiftness. The bottles hopper beside the stand is unlocked only in that half and the hopper under the stand only in the other, when it takes the three potions to the output chest (a repeater keeps the bottles hopper locked a moment longer when the halves change, so a fresh bottle is never taken out unbrewed). Blaze powder goes into the stand's side from the fuel chest.
