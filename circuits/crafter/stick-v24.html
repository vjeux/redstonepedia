Stick crafter: crafts by itself from a supply chest (autocrafter)

crafts 4 sticks from every 2 planks · 16 blocks

Crafts 4 sticks from every 2 planks by itself: fill its supply chest and every full set is crafted into its output chest (75.5 crafts a minute). A partial set waits, without jamming, until the rest arrives.

passed at every position and facing we tested · the first craft after 2 s · fits in 4×2×3

crafts 4 sticks from every 2 planksAuto-crafter3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

After building it, put a chest on top of its hopper (the supply) and one under it (the output): the download has neither (our test adds them, and they do not count in its blocks). Its crafters have slots disabled (all 9 slots and 7 slots): the download keeps them; building it by hand, disable the same slots by clicking them in the crafter's screen.

Try it

The view shows the crafter as built, without the items our test feeds it. In the game, fill the chest on top of its hopper with 2 planks (or more): every full set becomes 4 sticks in the chest below.

How this design does it: The iron block machine with seven of the crafter's slots disabled, so the planks fill the top two slots of the left column and the comparator reads 9 once two planks are in.

# CIRCUIT: stick-v24 crafter stick crafts/min 75.5, first craft by 40 gt, crafts 93 (2 waiting sets checked), hoppers awake 1, recipe class 2x2, supply items, bill 5 iron, 2 quartz, 12 redstone, 16 blocks, 4x2x3, robust
# Stick auto-crafter (clk-item 2026-10-21): the iron block layout (ironblock-v24) with seven of
# the crafter M's slots disabled (only the top two of the left column take planks), so its
# comparator reads 9 once two planks are in. The constant 9 at K's side (comparator J reads the
# crafter C, whose nine slots are all disabled, into the dust D) lets K turn on only then; two dust
# carry its signal to the repeater R, which powers the stone S beside M, and M crafts four sticks
# into the chest below.
