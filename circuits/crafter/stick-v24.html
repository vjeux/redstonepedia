Stick crafter: crafts by itself from a supply chest (autocrafter)

crafts 4 sticks from every 2 planks · 16 blocks

Crafts 4 sticks from every 2 planks by itself: fill its supply chest and every full set is crafted into its output chest (75.5 crafts a minute). A partial set waits, without jamming, until the rest arrives.

the first craft after 2 s · fits in 4×2×3

Try it

The view shows the crafter as built, without items. In the game, fill the chest on top of its hopper with 2 planks (or more): every full set becomes 4 sticks in the chest below.

How this design does it: The iron block machine with seven of the crafter's slots disabled, so the planks fill the top two slots of the left column and the comparator reads 9 once two planks are in.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

After building it, put a chest on top of its hopper (the supply) and one under it (the output): neither is in the download or its block count. Its crafters have slots disabled (all 9 slots and 7 slots): the download keeps them; building it by hand, disable the same slots by clicking them in the crafter's screen.
