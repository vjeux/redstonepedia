TNT crafter: crafts by itself from a supply chest (autocrafter)

crafts a TNT block from every 5 gunpowder and 4 sand · 31 blocks

Crafts a TNT block from every 5 gunpowder and 4 sand by itself: fill its supply chests and every full set is crafted into its output chest (18.8 crafts a minute). A partial set waits, without jamming, until the rest arrives.

passed at every position and facing we tested · the first craft after 4 s · fits in 5×3×5

Notes from its designer

Crafts a TNT block from every 5 gunpowder and 4 sand. Gunpowder and sand must go into the crafter in turn, because TNT's pattern alternates them and a crafter fills its slots in order. A comparator reads how many slots are filled; each change flips two copper bulbs, one locking the sand hopper while the count is even, the other locking the gunpowder hopper while it is odd. The second bulb also pulses the crafter, which crafts once all nine slots are full; an extra comparator keeps the sand hopper locked from the ninth item until the bulbs have flipped back after the craft.

# CIRCUIT: tnt-a crafter tnt crafts/min 18.8, first craft by 80 gt, crafts 21 (2 waiting sets checked), hoppers awake 1, recipe class multi, supply items.gunpowder + items.sand, bill 10 iron, 7 quartz, 27 redstone, 31 blocks, 5x3x5, robust
# TNT auto-crafter, first layout (clk-item 2026-10-21): gunpowder and sand must enter the crafter M
# alternately (TNT's pattern is G S G / S G S / G S G, and a crafter fills its slots in order).
# The comparator A reads M's filled slots onto the dust d; two observers watch d, and each change
# toggles both copper bulbs. B1 (lit while the count is even) locks the sand hopper through the
# comparator C1 and the stone beside the hopper; B2 (lit while it is odd) locks the gunpowder
# hopper through C2, the stone S2 and two dust, and the stone under that dust powers M: M tries a
# craft each time the count turns odd (a part of TNT's pattern crafts nothing) and crafts at 9.
# K compares M's count with a constant 9 (comparator J reading a crafter whose nine slots are
# disabled) and, through a repeater, keeps the sand hopper locked from the ninth item until the
# bulbs have turned back after the craft.
