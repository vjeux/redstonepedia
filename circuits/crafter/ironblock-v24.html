Iron block crafter: crafts by itself from a supply chest (autocrafter)

crafts an iron block from every 9 iron ingots · 16 blocks

Crafts an iron block from every 9 iron ingots by itself: fill its supply chest and every full set is crafted into its output chest (16.6 crafts a minute). A partial set waits, without jamming, until the rest arrives.

passed at every position and facing we tested · the first craft after 5 s · fits in 4×2×3

After building it, put a chest on top of its hopper (the supply) and one under it (the output): the download has neither (our test adds them, and they do not count in its blocks). One of its crafters has all 9 slots disabled: the download keeps them; building it by hand, disable the same slots by clicking them in the crafter's screen.

Notes from its designer

Crafts an iron block from every nine iron ingots. A hopper fills the crafter one ingot per slot. A comparator reads the crafter; a second comparator, reading a crafter whose nine slots are all disabled, holds a constant 9 at its side, so it turns on only when all nine slots are full. Its signal goes round through two dust and a repeater into the stone beside the crafter, which then crafts into the chest below.

# CIRCUIT: ironblock-v24 crafter iron_block crafts/min 16.6, first craft by 100 gt, crafts 22 (2 waiting sets checked), hoppers awake 1, recipe class 3x3, supply items, bill 5 iron, 2 quartz, 12 redstone, 16 blocks, 4x2x3, robust
# Iron block auto-crafter (clk-item 2026-10-21): the hopper H feeds the crafter M from the supply
# chest above H; M's comparator K (compare mode) has a constant 9 at its side (comparator J reads
# the crafter C, whose nine slots are all disabled, into the dust D beside K), so K turns on only
# once all nine of M's slots are filled. Two dust carry K's signal round to the repeater R, which
# powers the stone S beside M, and M crafts four game ticks later into the output chest below.
