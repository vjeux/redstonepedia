Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

The output is a copy of the input, N = 2 gt (0.1 s) later: a pulse goes in, the same pulse comes out N later, same length.

Pulses shorter than 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes) get lost. Two pulses closer than 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes) merge.

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: press and hold the IN button of the bar (or the cell tagged IN) to power that input; a quick tap sends a short 2 gt pulse.

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged OUT: the orange glass marker next to it glows while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

# CIRCUIT: delay-rep2-n0 delay N 2 gt, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), min-gap 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), tileable every 1 (0 0 1), 2 blocks, 1x2x1, robust
