Logic gate

A same as B · 17 blocks

It has two inputs, A and B. The output is on when A and B are the same: both on or both off.

passed at every position and facing we tested · reacts in 4 ticks (0.2 s) · made of comparator, redstone torch, redstone wire · fits in 5×4×2

Try it

Use the A and B buttons under the view to drive the inputs: the box marked OUT turns red while the output is on. Press play under the view to watch our test on a loop; touch any other control to take over.

How this design does it: XNOR gate, 17 blocks, 4 ticks: the 15-block XOR (a dust on each input block, two subtract comparators in a square with them) whose two signals meet on a stone instead of the output; a wall torch on that stone is the output, lit unless exactly one input is on.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A logic gate: the output depends only on the inputs right now. XNOR: the output is on while A and B are the same (both on or both off).

The output settles 4 gt (0.2 s) after an input changes.

How to try it

Press play under the view to watch the judge's own test on a loop (the badge reads demo with the step it is on; the same test is drawn as a timing diagram below). Touch any control to stop it and try the circuit yourself.

To try it: tap the A button of the bar (or the cell tagged A) to turn that input on or off (it powers the cell with a redstone block); tap the B button of the bar (or the cell tagged B) to turn that input on or off (it powers the cell with a redstone block).

Inputs are tagged in lime; the output is tagged OUT: the orange glass marker next to it glows while the output is on (the output strip by the demo badge shows its last 40 game ticks, 2 seconds). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, step one game tick at a time with the step button.

Our test in the real game

The judge drives the inputs with this schedule and checks the output after every change; here it is simulated (the 3D view's demo plays the same). Lime: an input is on; orange: the output is on (taller = a stronger signal, 1 to 15). Time runs left to right in game ticks (20 gt = 1 s).

Swipe the diagram sideways: the whole test lasts 281 ticks (14.05 s).
