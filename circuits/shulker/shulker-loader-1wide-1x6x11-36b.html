Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

It packs items into shulker boxes: it takes empty shulker boxes from the chest at its BOXES cell and items from the chest at its ITEMS cell, fills each box completely, and sends it away, with everything in it, to the chest at its OUTPUT cell. Nothing is lost or duplicated and nothing is left on the ground: three boxes' worth of 64-stack items (5184 cobblestone, 1728 a box) come out as three full boxes.

It moves 2.50 items per second: one box every 11 min 32.35 s; the first box arrives 11 min 33.05 s after the start.

It also handles these situations:

  • always-on: no lever: it runs by itself
  • 16-stack: 16-stack items too (snowballs, ender pearls, eggs: 432 a box)
  • unstackable: unstackable items too (tools, armour: 27 a box)
  • colours: boxes keep their colour and their name
  • restart: when the items pause and come back, it carries on by itself
  • top-up: fills half-used boxes too
  • box-starved: nothing is lost while the empty-box supply runs out; it resumes when boxes come back
  • drop-free: box items never lie on the ground
  • tileable(1):

How to try it

The machine runs in the view. The view runs it without end: the chests at the cells tagged ITEMS and BOXES are kept full (items, empty shulker boxes) and the one at OUT is emptied as boxes arrive. The items are unstackable (27 fill a box). In the view the first box goes out 12.65 s after the machine starts, then one every 11.85 s. The numbers over each chest, hopper and dispenser show what it holds. Speed it up with the bar's speed dropdown (up to 100×).

It starts by itself (no lever to flip). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
