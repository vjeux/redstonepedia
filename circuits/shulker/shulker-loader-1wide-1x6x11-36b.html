Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

It packs items into shulker boxes. The judge puts a chest of items at the design's ITEMS cell and a chest of empty shulker boxes at its BOXES cell; the machine places a box, fills it, and once it is completely full sends it away, with everything in it, to the chest at its OUTPUT cell. In the test the judge supplies three boxes' worth of 64-stack items (5184 cobblestone, 1728 a box) and all three full boxes must arrive, with no item lost or duplicated and nothing left on the ground, at 4 positions and 4 rotations in the real game.

It moves 2.50 items per second: one box every 13847 gt (11 min 32.35 s); the first box arrives 13861 gt (11 min 33.05 s) after the start.

It also passes these scenarios (each a separate run of the judge):

  • always-on: no lever: it runs by itself
  • 16-stack: 16-stack items too (snowballs, ender pearls, eggs: 432 a box)
  • unstackable: unstackable items too (tools, armour: 27 a box)
  • colours: boxes keep their colour and their name
  • restart: when the items pause and come back, it carries on by itself
  • top-up: fills half-used boxes too
  • box-starved: nothing is lost while the empty-box supply runs out; it resumes when boxes come back
  • drop-free: box items never lie on the ground
  • tileable(1):

How to try it

The machine runs in the view. The view runs it the way the judge does, without end: the chests at the cells tagged ITEMS and BOXES are kept full (items, empty shulker boxes) and the one at OUT is emptied as boxes arrive. The items are unstackable (27 fill a box). In the view the first box goes out 253 gt (12.65 s) after the machine starts, then one every 237 gt (11.85 s). The numbers over each chest, hopper and dispenser show what it holds. Speed it up with the bar's speed button (up to 100×).

It starts by itself (no lever to flip). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

# CIRCUIT: shulker-loader-1wide-1x6x11-36b shulker-loader boxes/min 0.087, items/s 2.50, first box 13861 gt, cycle 13847 gt, hoppers awake 4, badges always-on+16-stack+unstackable+colours+restart+top-up+box-starved+drop-free+tileable(1), bill 26 iron, 14 quartz, 48 redstone, 3 slime, 36 blocks, 1x6x11, robust
# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (36 blocks, 1x6x11): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: a comparator reads the box and a copper bulb toggles once per full box; the
# comparator reading the bulb starts a chain of sticky pistons and observers over the top that
# ends at a dropper above the breaking piston, and the observer under that dropper fires the
# piston. A comparator on the first hopper of the box line (it sees a box arrive even one at a
# time, or from a chest kept full) and the observer next to the breaker both fire a dispenser
# block; a sticky piston then moves an observer down, and observers and droppers pass the pulse
# to a dropper under the box dispenser, which fires it without touching the hoppers beside it.
# It passes the judge's full-chest, endless-supply, one-box and one-box burst checks. Found by
# `mctas circuit shulker anneal --sweep 23,29,37` (every trickle phase required) from the
# 41-block 1x6x11 loader (pext).
