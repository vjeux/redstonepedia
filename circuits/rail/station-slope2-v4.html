Notes from its designer

A two-way minecart station: the one-way station's two braking slopes, then a detector rail and an always-on booster. Carts going the station's way stop on the slopes and leave when the button is pressed. Carts coming the other way are sped up by the booster, cross the detector rail quickly, and it switches the slopes on long enough for them to climb straight through.
