Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A hopper minecart holds 320 items (5 stacks). A hopper under the cart pulls one item every 8 game ticks, 2.5 items a second, so an unloader is as fast as the hoppers that reach the cart: one under the cart empties a full one in about 2 minutes; two, by holding the cart where it straddles two blocks, in about 1. The station sends the cart back only once its hoppers find nothing more in it.

How to try it

The view shows the station as built, with our test's chest and no cart: the browser's simulator does not run hopper minecarts here. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom. Its numbers are our test's, in the real game, below.

Notes from its designer

The cart climbs a short powered slope and stops against a closed fence gate, which leaves it straddling two blocks: the hopper under the slope and the hopper beside it both pull from it, emptying a full cart in 64 seconds, twice as fast as one hopper. When the hoppers find nothing more, the slope turns on and the cart rolls back down the line. Pair it with ld-chest and 128 blocks of track: a double chest delivered in 21 minutes.

# CIRCUIT: ul-gate cart-unloader line terminus, unload rate 4.79 items/s, departure delay 14 gt, 31 blocks, 6x3x4, robust
# B-unloader (designs/briefs/cart-transport.md, layout B; the reviewer's proof first): boosters
# send the cart up the powered slope P toward a closed fence gate G. The detector rail D at the foot
# of the slope cuts P's power through a repeater as the cart reaches the top: P then halts it. Any
# rest point between 0.51 and 0.94 of P's cell puts the cart's box over two cells, so the hopper H1
# under P and the hopper H2 beside it (P's support, pulling from the gate's cell) both pull from the
# cart (HopperBlockEntity.getEntityContainer: a cell's box); the gate's plate (mid-cell) caps the
# climb at 0.885. Both push into the storage chest. While items flow, the comparator on H2 keeps the
# torch on Q off; when H2 stays empty the torch powers P through a repeater, and the cart rolls back
# down the line.
