Notes from its designer

A one-way minecart station: the track drops two blocks on two powered rails that slope down the way the carts go. Switched off, a powered rail brakes a cart hard, so every cart coming in, even at full speed, stops on the slope, ridden or empty. The button beside the lower rail (in reach from the cart) switches both rails on: the cart rolls down and is pushed out at speed.

# CIRCUIT: station-slope-v2 station 1 line one-way, stop distance 1.60 blocks, departure delay 1 gt, leaves at 0.22 b/t, 6 blocks, 3x3x2, robust
# station-slope-v2 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): a one-way minecart station.
# The line drops two blocks through it on two powered rails sloping down in the direction of travel
# (east). Unpowered, a powered rail halves a cart's speed every tick: a cart arriving with up to
# 2.0 b/t of momentum comes to rest on one of the two slopes (a slower one on the first). The
# button is a floor button on a stone beside the lower slope, below a rider's eyes wherever the
# cart rests (the judge checks a look ray from the cart reaches it). A press strongly powers that
# stone, which powers the lower slope, which powers the upper one: the cart slides down, the
# powered rails boost it, and it leaves east.
name: station-slope-v2
