Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A hopper minecart holds 320 items (5 stacks) and pulls one item a tick, 20 items a second, from a chest right above its rail: stopped under the supply chest, it fills in 16 seconds. The station sends it off when it is full, or when the chest has run dry and it holds something; an empty cart at an empty chest stays, so an idle farm's cart does not shuttle back and forth.

The view shows the station as built, with our test's chest and no cart: the browser's simulator does not run hopper minecarts here. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom. Its numbers are our test's, in the real game, below.

Notes from its designer

The cart stops under the supply chest and pulls one item every tick: 20 items a second, a full cart in 16 seconds. It leaves when it is full, or when the chest has run empty and it holds something; an empty cart at an empty chest waits there until items come, so an idle farm's cart does not shuttle. One comparator reads the cart's fill through the detector rail; its side input is the chest's own comparator lifted to full strength by a repeater, so it passes the fill only when the cart is full or the chest is empty, and then the slope under the cart turns on. Pair it with ul-gate and 128 blocks of track: a double chest delivered in 21 minutes.

# archived 2026-10-22T16:23Z from designs/circuits/tracks/cart-transport/ld-chest.txt by mctas circuit archive
# CIRCUIT: ld-chest cart-loader line terminus, load rate 16.87 items/s, departure delay 17 gt, 44 blocks, 9x4x6, robust
# The obvious fast loader (designs/briefs/cart-transport.md), dry-supply fix (coordinator and
# reviewer 2026-10-22): the hopper minecart parks under the supply chest and pulls one item per tick
# from it (MinecartHopper.tryConsumeItems: 20 items/s). Boosters send the cart up the halting
# powered slope P and the detector slope D; it rolls back and rests on P under the chest, where D
# still sees it (an open trapdoor over D stops the climb under the chest). One comparator C reads the
# cart's fill through D (compare mode); its side is "the chest has items" at full strength: the
# comparator K reads the chest through the block over D, the repeater after it lifts any signal to
# 15, and a dust line and a repeater bring it into C's side. So C passes the cart's fill only when
# the cart is full (15 against 15), or when the chest is empty and the cart holds something (fill
# against 0); C then powers P and the cart leaves down the line. An empty cart at an empty chest
# waits there until items come.
