Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

A hopper minecart pulls one item a tick, 20 items a second, from a chest right above its rail, and holds 320 items (5 stacks). At the loader it stops under the supply chest and fills; it leaves when it is full, or when the chest has run dry and it holds something. Empty, at an empty chest, it stays put, so an idle farm's cart does not shuttle back and forth. At the other end, the unloader holds it over hoppers that empty it into the storage chest, then sends it back.

A powered rail every 8 blocks, kept on by a redstone torch, keeps the cart at full speed both ways. A cart only moves in chunks the game is running: with a server's default simulation distance (10 chunks), a line of 128 blocks keeps both ends running while you stand at either one; a longer line works only while you stand near its middle.

How to try it

The view shows the line as built, with our test's two double chests: the supply chest at the loader's end and the storage chest at the unloader's. The browser's simulator does not run its cart yet. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom. In the game, put one hopper minecart on the track and items in the supply chest: the cart fills, carries them to the storage chest and comes back for more.
