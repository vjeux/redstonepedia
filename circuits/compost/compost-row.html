Auto-composter

1 composter a slice, one crop each · slices side by side · 18 blocks

Takes the crops out of a hopper line of mixed farm output and composts them, one crop a slice: build a slice for every crop your farm sends (each slice's filter hopper holds its crop); the bone meal drops into a chest under each composter and everything else runs on to the overflow chest at the end of the line, 111.4 items composted a minute.

Composting is random: each item fills the composter with its own chance (wheat seeds and kelp 30%, sugar cane 50%, wheat 65%), so the bone meal per crop varies from run to run; seven layers make one bone meal.

passed at every position and facing we tested · its first bone meal within 17 s · fits in 1×6×5

Notes from its designer

One composter a slice, as many slices side by side as there are crops to compost: the line of hoppers along the top carries the farm's mixed output; each slice's filter hopper holds its crop (41 of it and four named papers), so it takes only that crop from the line, and a comparator reading it unlocks the hopper under it for a moment whenever one more arrives. That hopper, kept full of the crop, drops one into the composter each time; the hopper under the composter pulls the bone meal out into the slice's chest. Anything no slice takes runs on to the overflow chest at the end of the line. A block on each line hopper keeps strays out (the input chest stands on the first).
