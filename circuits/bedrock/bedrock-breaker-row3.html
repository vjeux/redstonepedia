Bedrock breaker: removes 3 bedrock blocks side by side

3 bedrock · 21 blocks

Removes 3 bedrock blocks side by side in the Nether roof's top layer, from on top of the roof. The machine arms the piston standing on each bedrock once every 6 ticks (0.3 s); one key press at that moment (Attack and Use bound to the same key, the piston breaks and a piston facing down goes in its place) and the bedrock under it disappears. A press at the wrong moment is undone with one more press. You need an Efficiency V pickaxe and Haste II from a full beacon, so that the piston breaks instantly.

fits in 4×3×5

Try it

Press play, then press Press under the view (or the p key), or tap the piston standing on the bedrock: it breaks and a piston facing down goes in its place, as the key bound to Attack and Use does in the game. In an armed tick (one in every 6) the bedrock under it disappears; any other tick it stays and the piston is left facing down: tap it again to put it back. To hit the armed tick, pause and step one game tick at a time: it comes right after the piston's arm finishes extending.

How this design does it: Three bedrock side by side: the end pistons face out, the middle one faces up; each has its own stone and observer; the clock's redstone block slides behind the first two observers and the clock line runs on a block behind the third.
