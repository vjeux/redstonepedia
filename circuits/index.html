Circuits

The classic redstone contraptions, one collection each. Every design was tested in the real game; each page says what it does, why you would build it, and lets you try it in 3D.

Pulse shapers
One short pulse from a lever, long presses cut short, quick blips stretched.
Toggle switches (T‑flip‑flops)
One button that turns things on, then off.
Delays
The same signal, a set number of ticks later.
Logic gates
AND, OR, NOT, XOR: a door that needs two levers, a light switched from both ends.
Memory and counters
Remember an event until reset, or react on every 2nd, 3rd... pulse.
Piston extenders
Push a block 2 to 12 blocks out and pull it back exactly.
Flying machines
Machines that fly by themselves: trains, tree farms, world eaters.
Shulker box loaders and unloaders
Box a farm's output into shulker boxes, or unpack them into storage.
TNT dupers
Endless lit TNT, without crafting any.
Block change detectors (BUD switches)
Notice a block placed, removed or grown next to it.
Timers and pulse multipliers
One-shot timers, stay-on timers, several pulses per press, clock dividers.
Item sorters
Sort mixed items into chests, one kind per slice, slices side by side.
Combination locks
Open a door only when every item frame shows its code, or when its buttons are pressed in the right order.
Displays
Show a container's fill level as a 7-segment digit, 0 to 9.
Adders
Add bits: half adders, full adders, and slices that chain into any width.
Randomizers
One press lights one of several outputs at random: coin flips, dice, lotteries.
Crop farms
Self-harvesting crop farms: the whole farm's harvest an hour, and per block placed.
Auto-smelters
Furnaces fed from one input chest and one fuel chest, every result in one chest.
Auto-brewers
Brewing stands fed from chests, wart then sugar in order, finished potions in one chest.
Auto-composters
Composters fed from a farm's hopper line, one crop a slice, the bone meal in chests.
Item elevators
Lift items N blocks up from a chest into a chest, as fast as a hopper feeds them.
Minecart stations
Stop a cart at the platform, send it on with a button; load and unload hopper minecarts; carry a chest's items far away.
Auto-crafters
Crafters that craft by themselves from a supply chest: iron blocks, crafting tables, sticks.
Automatic doors
A 2×2 piston door that opens by itself as you walk up and closes behind you.
