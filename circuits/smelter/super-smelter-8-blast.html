Auto-smelter: furnaces fed from one input chest and one fuel chest

a double chest of raw iron in 36 min (100% of 8 blast furnaces) · ~8 stacks of coal · 108 blocks · rests

Smelts a double chest of raw iron (3,456) in 36 minutes with its 8 blast furnaces: dump the load in its input chest and the coal it needs plus one stack in its fuel chest (432 + 64 for a double chest), and every ingot ends up in its output chest. With the chest empty nothing moves: no clock runs on. Blast furnaces smelt only ores, raw metals and metal tools and armour.

Fuel it with coal or charcoal: other fuels, such as lava buckets or blocks of coal, may be shared out unevenly between the furnaces.

blast furnaces: twice as fast, ores and raw metals only; as many blocks as the main design · fits in 11×6×4

After building it, put a double chest at ITEMS (the items to smelt), a chest at FUEL (the coal) and a chest at OUT (the results): none of them is in the download or its block count.

Blocks used: blast furnace comparator hopper redstone torch redstone wire repeater stone

Fuel it from a farm: Crop farm: fuels ~2,750 items smelted an hour: a double chest of ore every 1.3 h, 95% of an 8-furnace super smelter at full speed · 639 bamboo stalks · 2,492 blocks · ~4,400 iron + 92 slime to build: half of this smelter at full speed.

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

spreads the items of its input chest over its furnaces, feeds them coal from its fuel chest and collects every result in its output chest

How to try it

The smelter runs in the view: items to smelt start in the chest tagged ITEMS and coal in the chest tagged FUEL; hoppers carry them into the furnaces, and every result drops into the chest at OUT. The numbers over each container show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed dropdown. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
