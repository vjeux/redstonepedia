Auto-smelter: furnaces fed from one input chest and one fuel chest

a double chest of raw iron in 36 min (100% of 8 blast furnaces) · ~8 stacks of coal · 108 blocks · rests

Smelts a double chest of raw iron (3,456) in 36 minutes with its 8 blast furnaces: dump the load in its input chest and the coal it needs plus one stack in its fuel chest (432 + 64 for a double chest), and every ingot ends up in its output chest. With the chest empty nothing moves: no clock runs on. Blast furnaces smelt only ores, raw metals and metal tools and armour.

Our test feeds it coal (charcoal works the same); other fuels such as lava buckets or blocks of coal may be shared out differently between the furnaces.

blast furnaces: twice as fast, ores and raw metals only; as many blocks as the pick of its size · fits in 11×6×4

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

spreads the items of its input chest over its furnaces, feeds them coal from its fuel chest and collects every result in its output chest

# CIRCUIT: super-smelter-8-blast smelter 8 minutes for a double chest 36.2, of the ceiling 100% (36.0 min: 8 furnaces always busy), first ingot by 240 gt, spread 432 each, coal left 64 (64 in the machine), at rest yes, hoppers awake 32, coal needed 496 (the load's 432 and a stack), bill 240 iron, 1 quartz, 30 redstone, 108 blocks, 11x6x4, robust
# B-8, self-clocked (designs/briefs/super-smelter.md, layout B). Per furnace: the input line hopper,
# a locked hopper that takes one raw iron per window, an open hopper under it that drains it into the
# furnace top, a locked side hopper that takes one coal per window from the fuel line, the output
# hopper. The last line hopper faces a comparator: when the line has filled up to it the comparator
# (through a 4 gt repeater: the window opens 8 gt after the last hopper fills, when every line
# hopper holds an item) turns the torch off for a few ticks, unlocking every locked hopper once.
# The next window opens when the line is full again; with the chest empty nothing moves: it rests.
