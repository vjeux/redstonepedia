Auto-smelter: furnaces fed from one input chest and one fuel chest

1 furnace · 4 blocks

Shares the items of its input chest out to its furnace, feeds them coal from its fuel chest and collects every result in its output chest, 6 items a minute per furnace.

Fuel it with coal or charcoal: other fuels, such as lava buckets or blocks of coal, may be shared out unevenly between the furnaces.

6.0 items a minute per furnace · its first result within 11 s · made of furnace, hopper · fits in 1×3×2

Try it

How this design does it: One furnace: the hopper under the input chest drops items into the furnace's top, the hopper under the fuel chest pushes coal into its side, and the hopper under the furnace takes each result down into the output chest.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

After building it, put a chest at ITEMS (the items to smelt), a chest at FUEL (the coal) and a chest at OUT (the results): none of them is in the download or its block count.

Blocks used: furnace hopper

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

spreads the items of its input chest over its furnaces, feeds them coal from its fuel chest and collects every result in its output chest

How to try it

The smelter runs in the view: items to smelt start in the chest tagged ITEMS and coal in the chest tagged FUEL; hoppers carry them into the furnaces, and every result drops into the chest at OUT. The numbers over each container show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed dropdown. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
