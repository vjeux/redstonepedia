Auto-smelter: furnaces fed from one input chest and one fuel chest

a double chest of raw iron in 36 min (100% of 16 furnaces) · ~8 stacks of coal · 221 blocks · rests

Smelts a double chest of raw iron (3,456) in 36 minutes with its 16 furnaces: dump the load in its input chest and the coal it needs plus one stack in its fuel chest (432 + 64 for a double chest), and every ingot ends up in its output chest. With the chest empty nothing moves: no clock runs on.

Our test feeds it coal (charcoal works the same); other fuels such as lava buckets or blocks of coal may be shared out differently between the furnaces.

Try it

Watch the hoppers carry the items into the top of each furnace and the coal into its side; the results drop into the output chest (the numbers over each container show what it holds).

How this design does it: Two 8-furnace super smelters back to back under a double chest of ore and a double chest of coal, each half feeding one row: every furnace takes exactly one raw ore and one coal each time its row's hopper line fills up, so all 16 furnaces work from the start and nothing runs once the chest is empty.

Details: how it works in depth, our test in the real game, measurements and properties

In depth

spreads the items of its input chest over its furnaces, feeds them coal from its fuel chest and collects every result in its output chest

How to try it

The smelter runs in the view the way our test does: items to smelt start in the chest tagged ITEMS and coal in the chest tagged FUEL; hoppers carry them into the furnaces, and every result drops into the chest at OUT. The numbers over each container show what it holds; speed it up with the bar's speed button. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Two 8-furnace super smelters back to back under a double chest of ore and a double chest of coal, each half feeding one row: every furnace takes exactly one raw ore and one coal each time its row's hopper line fills up, so all 16 furnaces work from the start and nothing runs once the chest is empty. Put a double chest of raw ore and 7–8 stacks of coal in, come back in 36 minutes. Variant: super-smelter-16-blast (blast furnaces: twice as fast, raw ores only).
