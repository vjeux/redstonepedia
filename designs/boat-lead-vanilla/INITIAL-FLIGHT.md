## Initial flight findings (fixed fixtures, not impossibility claims)

The first crossed ordinary-holder raft pair was genuinely attached by the survival client's lead + secondary-use interactions: A→llama-in-B and B→llama-in-A. Both ordinary llamas boarded by normal collision. A command-built flat support was removed at launch; no motion was injected. It did **not** sustain flight: each boat had 130 observed post-release ticks before void removal. Starting at y201, A/B ended around y-136/-137. The spring was active only 3/2 ticks, with zero undamped ticks; horizontal attraction collapsed the extension below slack, so lift ended. All observed flight samples retained server authority and loaded chunks. Leads remained until terminal removal. The server continued through 1226 ticks, but the apparatus did not survive to any later altitude window.

The one-player mixed pair successfully mounted a real survival player on an already seated tame unsaddled llama, with root/intermediate controllers null. Its first frozen-setup trial is **not a synchronized launch**: `TickRateManager.isEntityFrozen` allows entities carrying a player to tick despite `/tick freeze`, so the ridden root moved while the empty opposite root remained frozen. That asymmetric fixture produced some upward velocity and recurrent undamped phases, but still descended to the void in 366/376 post-release samples, not sustained flight.

The coordinator-approved normal-ticking physical-dock rerun removes the freeze ambiguity. Four fence posts around each raft held the horizontal layout while **both roots ticked normally** throughout genuine attachment and nested mounting. The exact graph and passengers were asserted before support removal. In `normal-dock-mixed-02`, both roots stayed server-authoritative, had no post-release supporting block/fluid samples or unloaded-chunk samples, and nevertheless descended to void removal in 136/135 samples. They did not sustain extension or altitude after release. Force-diagnostic reconstruction matched the observed leash delta to <=1.39e-16; 323 dry-air gravity phase samples matched exactly.

`normal-dock-mixed-01` is explicitly **invalid as the intended experiment**: the crosshair hit llamas instead of rafts, attaching and dismounting them. It is not evidence against the mixed graph. The driver now asserts crosshair target class, expected nested authority, actual passenger UUIDs, and both leash edges before launch, and stops on a mismatch.

These results establish the source mechanism and authority path, but not a useful flying machine. Horizontal extension and sustained favorable force phase remain the unresolved mechanism. The lower-seat/no-lead controls and the one approved opposite-yaw contrast also failed; final counts are in CONTROL-RESULTS.md. Per coordinator instruction the pair group stops here. No speed ceiling, universal impossibility, optimized design, or survival assembly/launch recipe is claimed.
