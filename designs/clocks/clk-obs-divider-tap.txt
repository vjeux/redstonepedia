# Extendable family divider (n = 0): P = 12 x 2^n gt, 5 + 3n blocks. An observer pair (6 gt) toggles the copper bulb behind it on every pulse (12 gt); each comparator reading a bulb powers the next bulb only every other pulse, doubling the period; the last comparator, reading the last bulb, is the output a builder wires (clockinfra 2026-10-23: the archived divider's output got the pair's 6 gt pulses, its bulb chain had no tap).
