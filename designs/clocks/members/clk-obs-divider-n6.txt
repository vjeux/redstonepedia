name: clk-obs-divider-n6
# Extendable family divider (n = 6): P = 12 x 2^n gt, 5 + 3n blocks. an observer pair (6 gt) toggles the copper bulb behind it on every pulse (12 gt); each comparator reading a bulb powers the next bulb only every other pulse, doubling the period; the last comparator, reading the last bulb, is the output a builder wires. Extension: add a stage behind the last bulb: a comparator (facing the clock, reading the bulb, on a stone floor) and a bulb; the tap at the end (a comparator reading the last bulb) moves back with it. Generator: mctas clock obs family divider --n N --out FILE.
