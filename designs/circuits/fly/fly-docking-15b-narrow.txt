# CIRCUIT: fly-docking-15b-narrow flying-machine two-way round trip 98 gt, switch docks within 87 gt, restarts within 0 gt, ground needs 1 block of air above the ground, 15 blocks, 4x2x9, robust
# Flying machine with docking stations, 15 blocks, and an on/off lever: a 6-block flying machine (two sticky pistons, two slime blocks, two observers) shuttles between two docking stations, 98 ticks per round trip. Each station has a wall that stops the machine and an observer that sees it arrive and fires into a dust behind the leading piston, which pushes the machine back the other way. At the station with the lever, the observer stands on a sticky piston: lever off pulls it aside, so the machine docks there and stays; lever on pushes it back into place, and a moved observer fires, so the docked machine leaves at once. It survives the lever flipped back too soon.
