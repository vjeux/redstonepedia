# CIRCUIT: brewer-small brewer 1 potions/min per stand 1.9, first potion by 1680 gt, blaze powder 1 for 8 brews, hoppers awake 9, at rest no, a clock runs on, blocks per stand 42.0, bill 60 iron, 3 quartz, 19 redstone, 42 blocks, 3x6x5, robust
# One brewing stand in a 3x6x5 box: a clock (two hoppers passing 106 stones back and forth, a
# copper bulb remembering the direction) splits time into two halves of 42 seconds. When the
# brewing half starts, the clock's own torch flashes under the block next to the bulb, which fires
# two droppers beside it: one lifts a nether wart into the hoppers over the stand, the other a
# sugar that takes two more hoppers and so arrives later, then waits until the wart is used up:
# the stand brews awkward potions, then swiftness. The bottles hopper beside the stand is unlocked
# only in that half and the hopper under the stand only in the other, when it takes the three
# potions to the output chest (a repeater keeps the bottles hopper locked a moment longer when the
# halves change, so a fresh bottle is never taken out unbrewed). Blaze powder goes into the
# stand's side from the fuel chest.
