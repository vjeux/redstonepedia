# Two comparator rings (crates/circuit-timer/src/long.rs). Stage 1 (two comparators, round 4 gt): each rising edge charges stage 1 with 4 gt of 15 (copper bulb, comparator K, observer O1 down into a stone and a cross dust, observer O2 watching O1 into the dust on top of I1); once its charge stops it decays 2 per round, so its output lasts at least 28 gt. A repeater reading its stone A1 powers stage 2's stone I2: stage 2 is a ring of 3 comparators (round 6 gt) that loses 1 per round, refilled evenly at 15 by stage 1's long output; once stage 1 dies it counts its levels down, and the output lamp beside its stone A2 goes dark at 0.
