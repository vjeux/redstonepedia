# a comparator ring (crates/circuit-timer/src/rings.rs): 3 dust carry C2's output back to C1, losing 2 per round of 4 gt; a rising edge charges it through a gate (copper bulb, comparator K, observer O1, a delay-3 repeater R, a subtract comparator Cg into I); a dust beside A and a repeater Rb feed Cg's side, so while the ring is on Cg passes nothing and every edge is ignored; R's 6 gt outlast the ring's 4 gt to raise Cg's side, so each charge ends when the ring blocks it
