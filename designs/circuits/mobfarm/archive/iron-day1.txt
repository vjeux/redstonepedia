# The day-1 iron farm: for 18 iron (the hopper, four buckets and the tripwire hooks) and no quartz, three villagers scared by a zombie summon iron
# golems that burn in lava pits, about 296 iron an hour.
# Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell cobblestone pods under cobblestone slabs; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks
# north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes. Golems spawn in a row of 8 1x1 pits west of
# the zombie, a lava source beside their head on both sides, and burn where they stand. One tripwire
# runs through every pit at knee height: a golem in any pit opens the trapdoor in front of the
# zombie, which hides it, and the villagers calm down (at night they fall asleep) while the golem
# burns. A water channel under the string carries the iron to one hopper.
# Hide the zombie by hand on the first night: the villagers need a recent sleep to summon the first
# golem. Built from day-1 materials: cobblestone, cobblestone slabs and torches (no glass, leaves or stone).
