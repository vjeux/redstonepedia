# The middle iron farm: for 13 iron and 6 quartz, four villagers summon a golem every 32 seconds,
# 443 iron an hour (93% of the game's ceiling), each golem dropping out of their sight at once.
# Four villagers sleep on beds in closed 1-cell pods under leaves (only three must agree, so a golem
# one of them sees does not stretch the cycle); a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in their view.
# At nightfall two inverted daylight detectors open the three trapdoors in front of the zombie for 46
# game ticks: the villagers fall asleep, then wake up scared for the rest of the night (the farm
# restarts by itself after a missed night). Golems spawn on a 6x6 pad; water from its corners pushes
# them onto the hole's ring (water over open fence gates: it holds the water, not a golem) and into
# its dry 2x2 center (open fence gates stop the water), and they fall 16 blocks out of every
# villager's sight to water that pushes them under a lava blade held by fence gates, a ceiling right
# above it, over two hoppers.
# Game check (the real 26.3 server, this file): 8 runs of 3 days, 114.5 golems/h (95% of the mean
# 113.1-115.9) against redsim's 112.3 (redsim reads 2% low); 451 golems by day and 465 by night;
# spawn cells chi-square 15.89 over 20 cells (5% critical value 30.1).
