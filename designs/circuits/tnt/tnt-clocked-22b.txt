# The TNT duper (vjeux 2026-10-20: one world-best implementation, built from catalog parts): 86 lit
# TNT a minute, standing right on the ground. Two catalog parts. The 16-block duplicator engine
# (tnt-triggered-launch-16b's, without its input block) makes one TNT each time its sticky piston
# loses power for a moment: held extended, released, it retracts, the redstone block on its slime
# frame comes back next to its dust and pushes it out again, which primes a TNT and throws it north
# at 1 block per tick. The clock is clk-obs-p14-lamp-obs-rb-stk-6b-b-0e0eb7 from the clock catalog
# (6 blocks, period 14 gt): its sticky piston moves a redstone block that sits next to the engine's
# piston (holding it) except for 4 ticks of every 14, so the engine dupes once per period. Each TNT
# lands and explodes on the ground 9 to 30 blocks away (7.4 to 27 on unbreakable ground), beyond
# the 6.9 blocks a blast can reach, so no explosion touches the machine and each one breaks ground
# blocks. Found by `mctas circuit tnt hold-scan` (every archived clock and turn, the cells where a
# piston is powered on all but one short window of the period, the engine's piston put there,
# judged on redsim); the 120-TNT-a-minute version (a period-10 clock) lands blasts as close as 5.3
# blocks and failed 1 of 16 game builds in 3 of 6 runs.
