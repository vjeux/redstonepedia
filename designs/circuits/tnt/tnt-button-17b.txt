# CIRCUIT: tnt-button-17b tnt-duper-triggered per pulse 1, latency 33 gt, gap 100 gt, safe drop 0 blocks (on the ground), 17 blocks, 3x4x4, robust (survives its own explosions: 6 exploded, design intact; useful: every blast clear of fluids, each destroys blocks on open ground)
# One TNT per BUTTON press (site QA 2026-10-21: a real button holds 20-30 gt, so the 16-block engine judged with a
# 4-tick removal of its holding redstone block lit several TNT per press). The engine of tnt-triggered-launch-16b with a
# one-shot hold instead: the obsidian beside the piston is strongly powered by a wall torch under it, which holds the
# piston; the torch hangs on the stone under the piston (in the obsidian foot's place) and the button is on that
# stone. A press powers the stone: the stone holds the piston itself and the torch goes dark. At the release the stone
# goes quiet 2 ticks before the torch relights: the piston drops, the frame comes back, its redstone block repowers
# the piston through the dust and the push lights ONE TNT, however long the press. Latency: the press, then 5 ticks.
