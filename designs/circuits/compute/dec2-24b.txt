name: dec2-24b
# A torch hung on each input gives its opposite. One wire touches both torches: it carries "a0 or a1
# is off", at full strength beside a lit torch and one level weaker a block away. Comparators in
# subtract mode each read an input or an opposite at the back and a wire at a side, and an output
# stays lit only while its comparator's side is weaker than its back, so exactly one of the four
# outputs is lit for each pair of inputs. (w86, 2026-10-21, `circuit arith synth --r7`.)

port input.a1: 2 1 -1
