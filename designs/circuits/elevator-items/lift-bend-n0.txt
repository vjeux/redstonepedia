# CIRCUIT: lift-bend-n0 item-elevator 5 items/s 2.50, first item 30 gt, at rest no, bill 5 iron, 7 quartz, 20 redstone, 14 blocks, 4x6x1, robust
name: lift-bend-n0
# A dropper elevator 5 blocks up (14 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of
# droppers facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest. One observer clock (6 gt) fires the
# column: its observer at the second dropper fires droppers 0-2 and leads (its partner is placed last:
# a player starts the clock by placing it); a four-observer bend from it fires droppers 3-5 4 gt later;
# then a chain of observers facing down climbs a block every 2 gt and an observer every two blocks fires
# the next three droppers 4 gt after the ones below (a hard-powered dropper powers the ones above and
# below it). A dropper also reads the power of the dropper two above (quasi-connectivity), so each
# group must be off whenever the group below turns off.
circuit: item-elevator 5
extend family: dropper-bend
extend axis: 5 + 2n
extend blocks: 14 + 5n
extend rule: two more droppers on the column, two more chain observers and one observer firing the new top group
extend n: 0..8
extend base: 0
extend generator: mctas circuit gen dropper-bend --n {n} --out {out}
port items: 0 1 0
port output: 1 6 0
port start: 3 1 0 last
origin: 0 0 0
