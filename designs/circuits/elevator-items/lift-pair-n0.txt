# CIRCUIT: lift-pair-n0 item-elevator 4 items/s 2.50, first item 26 gt, at rest no, bill 5 iron, 5 quartz, 15 redstone, 11 blocks, 4x5x1, robust
name: lift-pair-n0
# A dropper elevator 4 blocks up (11 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of
# droppers facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest. One observer clock (6 gt) fires the
# column: its observer at the second dropper is placed last (a player starts the clock by placing
# it), so its partner leads by 2 gt; a chain of observers facing down climbs from the partner a block
# every 2 gt, and an observer every two blocks fires the next three droppers 4 gt after the ones below
# (a hard-powered dropper powers the ones above and below it). A dropper also reads the power of the
# dropper two above (quasi-connectivity), so each group must be off whenever the group below turns off.
circuit: item-elevator 4
extend family: dropper-pair
extend axis: 4 + 2n
extend blocks: 11 + 5n
extend rule: two more droppers on the column, two more chain observers and one observer firing the new top group
extend n: 0..8
extend base: 0
extend generator: mctas circuit gen dropper-pair --n {n} --out {out}
port items: 0 1 0
port output: 1 5 0
port start: 2 1 0 last
origin: 0 0 0
