# CIRCUIT: tff-bulb tff latency 2 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), min-period 4 gt (the shortest a real input makes), tileable every 2 (0 0 2), 3 blocks, 2x2x1, robust
# T flip-flop, 3 blocks: the input powers a waxed copper bulb, which toggles on every rising edge of its power; a comparator on a stone reads the bulb and is the output. Latency 2 ticks, and it follows pulses every 4 ticks. For a toggle on the FALLING edge, hang the 1-block NOT (a wall torch) on the input block and let the torch power the bulb. A row of them tiles at pitch 2: at pitch 1 the comparators would sit side by side and each one's side input would suppress its neighbour (ComparatorBlock.getAlternateSignal).
