# Smallest always-on shulker unloader so far, 41 blocks (track shulker-speed, clk-item): clk-rt's
# always-on unloader without the hopper lock (its repeater, dust, stone and two floors) and without
# the drop cell's side walls and the wall south of the box cell: the unloading hopper is still on its 8 gt
# cooldown from the box's last item when the piston breaks the box, and the moving stone pushes
# the drop out of its reach in time (16 builds with 16 different drop draws). The dispenser
# above the box cell places full boxes, the hopper under it empties them; a squared comparator
# reading feeds a subtract comparator directly and 4 gt late (a pulse when the box runs empty,
# never on a new full box); a sticky piston two cells away pushes a stone into the box and the
# moving stone pushes the empty box's drop into a walled cell over the empties hopper; an observer
# on the pushed stone's cell fires the dispenser after each box; a comparator reading the
# dispenser through the drop cell's roof, watched by an observer, places the first box and
# restarts after the supply ran out.
