# Fastest always-on shulker loader, 7.4 items/s real fill rate (cycle 4681 gt per box of 1728 cobblestone: the ceiling of hoppers under 3 item chests; 3x the hopper loader; track shulker-speed, clk-item). The dispenser D above the box cell X places empty boxes; a sticky piston P breaks a full box and its stone pushes the drop into the cell over the collection hopper K. Items: chests a and b feed a pool hopper H1 (a from above, b through a side hopper); hopper N under H1 pulls from it and feeds X from the south; chest c feeds N directly through its south side, and hopper U under N pulls from N into the dropper Y under X (facing up); H1's push runs west and down a hopper chain into Y's west face. So N, which gives every new box its first item, never starves while any chest has items, and Y gets 5 items/s. Gate: comparator C reads X's level L through a stone into dust A; K2 (compare, redstone block beside it) passes only a full box, to the piston through two delay-4 repeaters and onto the side of subtract comparator Z, so Z passes L only while 0 < L < 15; repeater W squares it into the rear of G, a subtract comparator looping through repeater R into its own side (an 8 gt clock while the gate is open). Two observers fire Y: Q under Y watches G (it catches G's on-change; the off-change comes while Q is still on) and a second observer under the stone north of Y watches a delay-2 repeater on G's output, which changes 4 gt later; together they give Y a rising edge every 4 gt, 5 items/s. Y never fires at an empty cell, a breaking box or air (a dropper facing a full box keeps its item). Always-on: comparator E reads D and observer Q2 fires D when D's contents go between empty and not; the dispenser is powered through a stone so Y is not QC-fired by it. Shrunk in the game from 108 blocks (with `--min-rate`): two drop-cell walls and an unused delay line from Q2 into the gate are not needed.
