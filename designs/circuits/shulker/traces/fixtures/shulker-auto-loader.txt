# Always-on shulker loader (track shulker, hand-built). A comparator reads the dispenser through
# the drop cell's roof; an observer above that comparator fires the dispenser whenever it goes from
# holding no box to holding one, so it places the first box by itself and again whenever boxes come
# back after the supply ran out. The dispenser sits above the box cell (the drop, which pops at a
# random offset and drifts, cannot leave upward); a hopper feeds the box from the side; a
# comparator reads it through a stone, in compare mode with a redstone block on its side, so it
# outputs 15 only when full; through a stone, two dusts and a repeater it powers a stone beside a
# sticky piston two cells away (so the powered dispenser never quasi-powers it), which pushes a
# stone into the box: the box breaks and the moving stone pushes the drop into the walled drop cell,
# over the hopper to the output chest. An observer on the pushed stone's cell fires the dispenser
# for the next box. The floor under the box cell and the drop cell's north wall look removable
# (`circuit shulker shrink` took them out, and most runs pass), but a drop that drifts past them
# falls out of the machine now and then: they stay.
