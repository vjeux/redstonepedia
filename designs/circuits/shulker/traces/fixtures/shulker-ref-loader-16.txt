# Reference shulker loader (track shulker, hand-built). The dispenser sits ABOVE the box cell X (so
# the drop, which pops at a random offset and rises, can never leave X upward); a hopper feeds X
# from the side. A comparator reads X through a stone, in compare mode with a redstone block on its
# side, so it outputs 15 only when X is completely full; through a stone, two dusts and a repeater
# it powers a stone beside a sticky piston, which pushes a stone into X: the box breaks and drops
# with its contents, and the moving stone pushes the drop into the walled drop cell, over the
# hopper that sends it to the output chest. The piston is two cells from X so that the powered
# dispenser never powers it (quasi-connectivity). An observer watching the pushed stone's cell
# fires, through a stone, a dust on the dispenser, which places the next box from its hopper. A
# start lever, watched by a second observer and delayed 8 gt by a repeater (the first box needs
# 9 gt to reach the dispenser), places the first box.
