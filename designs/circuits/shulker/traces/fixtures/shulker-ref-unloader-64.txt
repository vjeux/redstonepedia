# Reference shulker unloader (track shulker, hand-built). The dispenser above the box cell X places
# a full box; the hopper under X empties it into the items chest. A comparator reads X through a
# stone; a repeater squares it (15 while the box holds anything) onto a dust that feeds a subtract
# comparator twice: directly on its side, and 4 gt late on its rear. When the box runs empty, the
# side drops first, so the subtract comparator gives a 4 gt pulse (never on a new full box: then
# the side rises first). The pulse fires a sticky piston that pushes a stone into X: the empty box
# breaks and drops, and the moving stone pushes the drop into the walled drop cell, over the hopper
# that sends it to the empties chest. The same pulse, 2 gt later through a repeater, locks the
# unloading hopper so it cannot pick the drop up. An observer watching the pushed stone's cell
# fires, through a stone, a dust on the dispenser for the next box. A start lever, watched by a
# second observer and delayed 8 gt by a repeater, places the first box.
