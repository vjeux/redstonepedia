# Mixed-item shulker loader, the first with the mixed badge (track shulker-speed, clk-item): clk-rt's flush loader plus a jam detector. Comparator H reads the feeding hopper N; besides the flush observer G, H drives four dust whose power decays to L_N - 3, so the fourth dust is on only when N's level reaches 4 (a backlog: N's items stopped entering the box, which only happens when the box has no empty slot). Subtract comparator Vj turns its rising edge into a 4 gt pulse (its side gets the same signal 4 gt late through repeater Rs), and the pulse powers, through a dust and a stone, the sticky piston that breaks the box.
