# Always-on shulker unloader, 2 hopper minecarts on an activator rail over 3 hoppers (track shulker-speed, clk-item). The dispenser D above the box cell X places full boxes. In the cell under X an activator rail runs east-west on top of the middle of three hoppers; two hopper minecarts stand still on it at the rail cell's two ends, each straddling two hoppers (a hopper pulls from every container entity whose box overlaps the cube above it) and each sucking X at 20 items/s, so the three hoppers drain X at 7.5 items/s into three item chests. The carts face 45 degrees so they never push each other. Empty detection as in the reference: comparator C reads X through a stone, repeater W squares it, subtract comparator V (side direct, rear 4 gt late through T) pulses when X becomes empty. The pulse powers, in the same tick, sticky piston P (its stone breaks the box and pushes the drop north into the walled drop cell over hopper K) and, through two dust under P, the activator rail, which disables both carts before they can suck the drop. Observer Q on the stone's cell re-fires D on the retraction. Comparator E reads D through the drop cell's roof and observer Q2 above it fires D whenever D's contents change: it places the first box by itself and again when boxes come back after the supply ran out.
