# CIRCUIT: fa3d-36b adder 1 latency 18 gt (a0 rises from a=0 b=1 cin=1), glitches 11, carry in and out, 36 blocks, 4x4x4, robust
name: fa3d-36b
# Full adder in a 4x4x4 cube, 36 blocks: two half adders stacked. The bottom one adds a and b, and its sum
# climbs a redstone dust onto the top one, which adds the carry-in and gives the sum. Each half adder lights its
# own carry block, and the carry-out lamp sits between the two blocks, so it lights when either carry does.
# (A glass block above the bottom carry torch keeps that torch from lighting the lamp. w86, 2026-10-21.)
circuit: adder 1
port input.a0: 3 1 -1
port input.b0: 2 1 3
port input.cin: 3 3 -1
port output.s0: 3 3 2
port output.cout: 0 2 2
