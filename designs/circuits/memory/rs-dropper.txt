# CIRCUIT: rs-dropper rs-latch latency 6 gt, tileable every 2 (0 2 0), 4 blocks, 2x2x2, robust
# Dropper RS latch, 4 blocks (track memory): two droppers face each other and hold one stone between them. s (next to the dropper by S) fires that dropper and the stone goes to the other one; r fires the dropper by R and it comes back; firing the empty one does nothing, so repeated s or r change nothing. A comparator on stone reads the dropper by R: q is its output (1). Latency 6 gt (dropper 4 + comparator 2). A stack of them tiles with NO gap (`tile: 0 2 0`): a column of latches costs 4 blocks per bit.
