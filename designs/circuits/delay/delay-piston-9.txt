# CIRCUIT: delay-piston-9 delay N 9 gt, min-pulse 6 gt, min-gap 5 gt, 10 blocks, 7x2x6, robust
# Delay line, 9 ticks, 10 blocks: the odd delays a repeater line cannot make. Two stages of two sticky pistons with redstone blocks, joined by a repeater set to 2. In each stage the first piston pushes its block against the second piston, which pulls its own block away from the next part. A piston set off by a block ARRIVING acts one tick later than one set off by a signal, so each stage takes 3 ticks one way and 2 the other, and inverts; two stages in a row add up to the same odd number on both edges.
