# CIRCUIT: pext-n3-flipsafe50-9x5x6 piston-extender 3 N 3, extend 55 gt, retract 55 gt, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 50 blocks, 9x5x6, robust
# A 3-piston extender you can flip back at any time (50 blocks, 9x5x6): the block goes out 3 cells
# in 55 gt and back in 55 gt, and turning the lever off or on again early never strands a block.
# How it works: it grew out of the slime-free stack of pext-n3-sf-v1, three sticky pistons in a
# row: going out each one pushes the ones in front of it, and coming back a logic of observers and
# pistons runs the single-cell moves it takes to pull every piston and the block home, one
# at a time. `mctas circuit pext anneal --flip-soft` (each failed flip test of the real-use audit,
# at all 4 rotations, costs more than any size) reworked that logic, adding a slime block, until
# every early flip ends extended or at rest (pext).
