# CIRCUIT: pext-n2-sand7-flipsafe-3x5 piston-extender 2 N 2, extend 8 gt, retract 12 gt, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 7 blocks, 3x5x1, robust
# A 2-piston extender you can flip back at any time (7 blocks, 3x5x1, one block thick): it lifts a
# sand block 2 cells (out in 8 gt, back in 12 gt), and turning the lever off or on again early
# never strands a block.
# How it works: the lever drives a small piston beside the column and, through the cell above it,
# the upper piston. An observer watching the small piston's head fires once at each lever flip and
# gives the sticky piston at the bottom a short pulse: when the upper piston is home, the pulse
# lifts it and the sand one cell and leaves them there, and the upper piston, still powered by the
# lever, pushes the sand one more; when the upper piston is up, the pulse pulls it back down, and
# the sand falls home. Found by `mctas circuit pext anneal --flip-soft` from pext-n2-sand6 (pext).
name: pext-n2-sand7-flipsafe-3x5

port payload: 0 2 0
origin: 0 0 0
