# CIRCUIT: pext-n2-flat-flipsafe10-6x4 piston-extender 2 N 2, extend 9 gt, retract 15 gt, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 10 blocks, 6x4x1, robust
# A 2-piston extender you can flip back at any time, sideways and one block thick (10 blocks,
# 6x4x1): the block goes out 2 cells in 9 gt and back in 15 gt, and turning the lever off or on
# again early never strands a block.
# How it works: the lever drives the back sticky piston, which pushes a second sticky piston and a
# slime frame carrying a third sticky piston, the block and an observer. Each time the frame lands,
# its observer gives the third piston a short pulse through the slime: going out it pushes the block
# one more cell and leaves it there; coming back it pulls the block home. On the way back the back
# piston only pulls the second piston home; an observer above that piston sees it arrive and gives it
# a short pulse that reaches the frame and pulls it home. Found by `mctas circuit pext anneal
# --flip-soft` (the flip tests at all 4 rotations in the energy) from pext-n2-rider-flat (pext).
