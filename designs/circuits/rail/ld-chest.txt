# CIRCUIT: ld-chest cart-loader line terminus, load rate 16.87 items/s, departure delay 17 gt, 44 blocks, 9x4x6, robust
# The obvious fast loader (designs/briefs/cart-transport.md), dry-supply fix (coordinator and
# reviewer 2026-10-22): the hopper minecart parks under the supply chest and pulls one item per tick
# from it (MinecartHopper.tryConsumeItems: 20 items/s). Boosters send the cart up the halting
# powered slope P and the detector slope D; it rolls back and rests on P under the chest, where D
# still sees it (an open trapdoor over D stops the climb under the chest). One comparator C reads the
# cart's fill through D (compare mode); its side is "the chest has items" at full strength: the
# comparator K reads the chest through the block over D, the repeater after it lifts any signal to
# 15, and a dust line and a repeater bring it into C's side. So C passes the cart's fill only when
# the cart is full (15 against 15), or when the chest is empty and the cart holds something (fill
# against 0); C then powers P and the cart leaves down the line. An empty cart at an empty chest
# waits there until items come.
