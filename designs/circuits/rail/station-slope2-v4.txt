# CIRCUIT: station-slope2-v4 station 2 line two-way, stop distance 1.60 blocks, departure delay 1 gt, leaves at 0.37 b/t, other way passes at 0.40 b/t, 10 blocks, 5x3x2, robust
# station-slope2-v4 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): a two-way minecart station.
# Two powered rails sloping down eastward: a cart arriving eastbound comes to rest on them
# (unpowered, a powered rail halves a cart's speed every tick). The button is a floor button on a
# stone beside the lower slope, below a rider's eyes wherever the cart rests; a press powers that
# stone, which powers both slopes: the cart slides down, crosses the detector rail and is boosted
# again by the always-on powered rail on the redstone block. A cart coming the other way
# (westbound) is boosted by that rail first, so it crosses the detector rail fast: the detector (on
# for 20 gt after it last saw the cart) powers both slopes long enough for the cart to climb
# through them. The detector rail also keeps the booster's power from running into the slopes
# (powered rails pass power only to powered rails).
