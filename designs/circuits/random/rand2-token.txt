# CIRCUIT: rand2-token randomizer 2 style hold, latency 8 gt, presses 48, 19 blocks, 10x3x1, robust (uniform: chi2 0.2 (limit 10.8) over 768 presses in 16 runs, counts 378/390)
# Randomizer 2, token pair, hold style (clk-item 2026-10-21): two droppers face each other, one holds an iron shovel (the token), the other a cobblestone. A press fires both in the same tick (the dust on the first one powers it and, pointing at the cell above the second, fires that one too): whichever fires first hands its item over, and the other then fires one of its two items back at random, so the shovel ends in either dropper with chance 1/2 whatever came before. A comparator reads each dropper (2 with the shovel, 1 with the cobblestone); two dusts drop that to 1 or 0 and a repeater (the tap) lights its output while the shovel is there.
