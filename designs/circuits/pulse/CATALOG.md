**long pulse limiter: a comparator and a loop of repeaters** ([base design](archive/pulse-limiter-u4.txt)) · 10 + 4n blocks · pulse-limiter

### L = 30 + 16n gt

**long pulse on both edges: an observer, two copper bulbs and a loop of repeaters** ([base design](archive/pulse-dual-edge-reset.txt)) · 33 + 4n blocks · dual-edge

Why a chain of repeaters: it answers both edges of inputs from 2 seconds (40 ticks) at any length; the smaller 13-block hopper version (a copper bulb holding a hopper item timer open) needs inputs about twice as long as its pulse, since an edge that comes while its items are moving cuts the pulse short.

n = 0: L = 30 gt, 33 blocks · n = 1: L = 46 gt, 37 blocks

Extend: lengthen the repeater U by one row: one more delay-4 repeater (on stone) in each column. n = 0..10 (L = 30 gt .. L = 190 gt); judged in the game: L = 30 gt, 46 gt, 62 gt, 78 gt, 94 gt, 110 gt, 126 gt, 142 gt, 158 gt, 174 gt, 190 gt.
