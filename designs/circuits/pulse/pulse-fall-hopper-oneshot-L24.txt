group title: falling-edge pulse of any length: a copper bulb and a hopper item timer
group note: L = 8 gt per item in the first hopper: one 13-block design for every L of the form 8n (8 gt to 24 s with 60 items)
# One pulse of exactly 8 ticks per item in the first hopper (8 ticks to 24 seconds) every time the
# input turns OFF, always 13 blocks: a copper bulb remembers the falling edge (the input makes a
# piston lift an observer away from the bulb, and when it turns off the piston pulls the observer
# back beside the bulb, where it fires once); while the bulb is lit, its comparator locks the
# second hopper and unlocks the first, so the items pass one by one; when the first hopper is
# empty, its comparator goes off and a torch toggles the bulb off; then the items flow back. This
# one holds 4 items: 32 ticks.
# Detail: the bulb's comparator powers the stone above the second hopper (locking it) and turns
# off the torch on that stone, which locked the first hopper; the first hopper's comparator powers
# the block whose torch lights the bulb from below when that hopper empties.
