# CIRCUIT: gate-xor gate xor function xor, latency 2 gt, 15 blocks, 4x4x2, robust
# XOR gate, 15 blocks, 2 ticks, glitch-free: a dust lies on each input block, and two subtract comparators stand in a square with them, so each dust is one comparator's back and the other's side. The comparator reading A's dust from behind has B's dust at its side and lights only when A is on alone; the other one, the mirror, lights only when B is on alone. The first points straight into the output; the second powers a block whose dust climbs over the square, across stones laid on the core, and lands on top of the output.
