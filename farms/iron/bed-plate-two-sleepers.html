Day-1 iron farm (iron armour on your first day)

344 iron an hour · 543 blocks · 0.63 an hour per block · 8 iron to build · bed-plate farm with two watchers and two sleepers, dug 11 deep

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

To build it: 8 iron in hand, 1 bucket of lava and 5 villagers from the village. Starts by itself the first night. Restarts by itself when you come back after days away.

44 minutes from the first block to a full set of iron armour, then 344 iron an hour · needs no redstone

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: 1 module(s); each: 2 scared and 2 blind villagers in 1-cell pods, a zombie in a boat, the bed plate; golems spawn on a 6x6 pad 7 below the beds and fall through its dry hole into a pit corner 19 below the beds under one lava source.

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
