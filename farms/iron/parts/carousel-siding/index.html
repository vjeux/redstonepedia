Bed siding

The one bed of the farm, beside the loop: each night every villager's cart turns off into it by turns; the villager is dropped beside the bed, sleeps, is woken by a zombie behind an iron hatch that opens and shuts by itself, gets back into its own cart, and waits there until its group passes again.

Part of the villager carousel, an iron farm still being built: this part as it stands today, with what it measured.

Real redstone; tested on its own; arrivals and release are driven by the test bench; not yet joined to the loop.

Bed siding on its test bench: the bench launches each villager's cart in and releases it; the siding's own redstone switches the track, the hatch and the drop, and sends a cart out if the scheduler's force signal comes while its villager is still awake; all 21 villagers share the one bed, set up by hand at build time
