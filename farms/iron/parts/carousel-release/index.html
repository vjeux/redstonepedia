Release controller

The redstone that sends the villagers out 3 by 3: each golem pulse releases the 3 head carts, then moves the 18-cart queue up 3 slots in three steps; a missed golem falls back to a timed release, and an early one is held.

Part of the villager carousel, an iron farm still being built: this part as it stands today, with what it measured.

Real redstone; tested on its own; its golem input is driven by the test bench and its outputs light lamps; not yet connected to the queue.

Sends the queue's front 3 villagers to the station after each golem, then moves the queue up 3; ignores a repeated signal, holds an early golem until the queue has moved, sends them anyway if a slot gets no golem, and does nothing until the first golem after it is built. Build step: put 23 stone in its hopper
