Middle-ground iron farm (the most iron for an evening's build)

430 iron an hour · 2,562 blocks · 0.17 an hour per block · 333 iron to build · villager carousel: twelve villagers ride minecarts past one golem station, a gate at every stop

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Twelve villagers ride minecarts on three loops; 57,319 blocks of room (43×31×43). Four three-villager modules side by side make 426 iron an hour each, at 28.0 build minutes each. Nether quartz: 56; iron: 333. Its storage holds 5 hours of iron.

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Carousel: 4 groups of 3 villagers, one per minecart, on 3 concentric loops (inner side 28); the golem's fall through a tripwire releases every cart at once to its next stop; the north stops are the station over the pad.

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Carousel: 4 groups of 3 villagers, one per minecart, on 3 concentric loops (inner side 28); the golem's fall through a tripwire releases every cart at once to its next stop; the north stops are the station over the pad. At dusk each cart drops its villager onto its stop's bed and waits there empty; at dawn it picks the villager up from a pocket past the brake.
