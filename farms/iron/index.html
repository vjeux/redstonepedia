Components

The pieces an iron farm is made of, each with the best versions to build into your own farm.

Farm module
One whole farm, the unit bigger farms are copies of: its iron an hour, its blocks and its build time. 9 versions.
Villager cage
Where the villagers sleep and watch the zombie: how many villagers, and how many of them watch. 14 versions.
Sleep trigger
What hides the zombie at night, so the villagers can sleep. 3 versions.
Golem spawn area
The floor where the golems appear: its size, and how often a golem finds a spot on it. 3 versions.
Golem drop
How the golems get from where they appear to where they die. 6 versions.
Kill chamber
Where the golems die: the lava, the drop, and whether the villagers see it. 7 versions.
Collection
How the iron reaches your chest, and how long the chest holds it. 2 versions.
Storage
The chests that hold the iron until you come back for it. 2 versions.
Access
The way to the chest and back, and the way in to build it. 1 version.

Villager carousel (in progress)

An iron farm being built: batches of villagers ride to one station by turns, and each batch summons its golem there. Each part as it stands today, with what it measured.

Station, portal pad and Nether kill
Where each batch of villagers panics and summons its golem: the golem lands on a pad touching a portal, leaves for the Nether at once, dies there in lava, and its iron is sorted and stored. 1 version.
Release controller
The redstone that sends the villagers out 3 by 3: each golem pulse releases the 3 head carts, then moves the 18-cart queue up 3 slots in three steps; a missed golem falls back to a timed release, and an early one is held. 3 versions.
Bed siding
The one bed of the farm, beside the loop: each night every villager's cart turns off into it by turns; the villager is dropped beside the bed, sleeps, is woken by a zombie behind an iron hatch that opens and shuts by itself, gets back into its own cart, and waits there until its group passes again. 1 version.
Single-lane loop
The villagers ride one track in carts: the queue moves up 3 places after each golem, the front 3 leave for the station and come back to the tail. 1 version.
