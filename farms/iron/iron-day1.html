Day-1 iron farm (iron armour on your first day)

296 iron an hour · 198 blocks · 1.5 an hour per block · 18 iron to build · golems burn in lava pits

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

To build it: 18 iron in hand, 16 buckets of lava and 3 villagers from the village. Hide the zombie by hand the first night. Needs the zombie hidden by hand after days away.

44 minutes from the first block to a full set of iron armour, then 296 iron an hour · needs no redstone

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell cobblestone pods under cobblestone slabs; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water and the lava (16 bucketfuls) where the view shows them. On the first night, hide the zombie from the villagers by hand (a block in front of it) and take the block away in the morning: the villagers need one night's sleep before they summon the first golem.

Notes from its designer

The day-1 iron farm: for 18 iron (the hopper, four buckets and the tripwire hooks) and no quartz, three villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems that burn in lava pits, about 296 iron an hour. Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell cobblestone pods under cobblestone slabs; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes. Golems spawn in a row of 8 1x1 pits west of the zombie, a lava source beside their head on both sides, and burn where they stand. One tripwire runs through every pit at knee height: a golem in any pit opens the trapdoor in front of the zombie, which hides it, and the villagers calm down (at night they fall asleep) while the golem burns. A water channel under the string carries the iron to one hopper. Hide the zombie by hand on the first night: the villagers need a recent sleep to summon the first golem. Built from day-1 materials: cobblestone, cobblestone slabs and torches (no glass, leaves or stone).
