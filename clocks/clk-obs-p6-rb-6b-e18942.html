Clock

every 6 ticks (0.3 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 6 ticks (0.3 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: there is no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of redstone block · on for 1 of 6 ticks

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: changing in place: redstone_block (piston, slime block, sticky piston only moved or static).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 27 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 9 ticks (0.45 s); then it repeats every 6 ticks (0.3 s), on for 1 tick (0.05 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
