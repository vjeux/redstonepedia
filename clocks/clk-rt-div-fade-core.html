Clock

every 228 ticks (11.4 s) · 34 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 228 ticks (11.4 s), forever. It starts on its own once built. To make it slower: append one master-slave T flip-flop stage (18 blocks) to the chain on the P114 comparator fading ring: the period doubles; each step adds 228 ticks (11.4 s) and 18 blocks, up to every 3648 ticks (3 min 2.4 s).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 460 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 228 ticks (11.4 s), on for 114 ticks (5.7 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
