Clock

every 35 ticks (1.75 s) · 15 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 35 ticks (1.75 s), forever. It starts on its own once built. To make it slower: one more delay-4 repeater on a stone in each arm of the hairpin (the dust U-turn moves one block out); each step adds 16 ticks (0.8 s) and 4 blocks, up to every 195 ticks (9.75 s).

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it.

made of calibrated sculk sensor, note block, observer, redstone wire, repeater · also works with trapdoor · on for 10 of 35 ticks

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 151 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 46 ticks (2.3 s); then it repeats every 35 ticks (1.75 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
