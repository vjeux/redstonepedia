Clock

every 14 ticks (0.7 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 14 ticks (0.7 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Stops for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back): start it again the way you started it after building it.

made of copper bulb, observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · 1 other block choice on its page · on for 2 of 14 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 14 ticks (0.7 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: P28: 1 copper bulb, 2 observers, 1 piston, 1 redstone block, 2 sticky pistons; changing in place: copper_bulb, observer, piston, redstone_block, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 54 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 12 ticks (0.6 s); then it repeats every 14 ticks (0.7 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
