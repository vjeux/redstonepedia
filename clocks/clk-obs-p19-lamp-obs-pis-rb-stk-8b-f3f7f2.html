Clock

every 19 ticks (0.95 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 19 ticks (0.95 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 8 of 19 ticks
