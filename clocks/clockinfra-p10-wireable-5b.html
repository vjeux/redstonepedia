Every clock that pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A 10-tick clock a builder can wire, one block over the 4-block record: a repeater facing out of its output cell takes every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 34 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 10 ticks (0.5 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
