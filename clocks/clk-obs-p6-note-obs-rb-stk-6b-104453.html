Clock

every 6 ticks (0.3 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 6 ticks (0.3 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of note block, observer, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 2 of 6 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 6 ticks (0.3 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: P6: 1 note block, 1 observer, 1 redstone block, 3 sticky pistons; changing in place: note_block, observer, redstone_block, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 31 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 6 ticks (0.3 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
