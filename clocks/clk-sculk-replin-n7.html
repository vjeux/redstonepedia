A longer version of the 9-block clock that pulses every 31 ticks (1.55 s): 4 steps longer. Each step: one more delay-4 repeater on a stone at the end of the line (the observer and note block move one block out): +8 ticks (0.4 s) of delay and +1 tick (0.05 s) of travel, which adds 9 ticks (0.45 s) and 2 blocks.

Every clock that pulses every 67 ticks (3.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 271 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 70 ticks (3.5 s); then it repeats every 67 ticks (3.35 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
