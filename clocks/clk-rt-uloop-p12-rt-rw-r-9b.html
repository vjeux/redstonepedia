Every clock that pulses every 12 ticks (0.6 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: U-loop: block A + dust beside it -> top row [delay-1 repeater (powered at placement)] -> block F -> a wall torch on F -> bottom row [delay-1 repeater (powered at placement)] -> A.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 36 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 12 ticks (0.6 s), on for 6 ticks (0.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
