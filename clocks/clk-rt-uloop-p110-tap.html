Clock

every 110 ticks (5.5 s) · 22 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 110 ticks (5.5 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse.

made of comparator, redstone torch, redstone wire · on for 106 of 110 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 110 ticks (5.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 334 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 110 ticks (5.5 s), on for 106 ticks (5.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
