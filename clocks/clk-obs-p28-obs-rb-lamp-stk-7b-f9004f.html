Clock

every 28 ticks (1.4 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 28 ticks (1.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 14 of 28 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 90 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 28 ticks (1.4 s), on for 14 ticks (0.7 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
