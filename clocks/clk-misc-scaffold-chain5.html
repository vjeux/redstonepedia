Every clock that pulses every 9 ticks (0.45 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: A scaffolding delay line: a chain of 5 scaffolding blocks hangs from scaffolding S1 (on stone); a sticky piston toggles a stone under the first one (2 gt observer pulses spit / pull it), so the chain's distances shift by one (0..4 <-> 1..5), one scaffold per gt (1 gt scaffolding ticks); an observer watches the last one, its back quasi-powering the piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 30 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 3 ticks (0.15 s); then it repeats every 9 ticks (0.45 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
