Clock

every 10 ticks (0.5 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 10 ticks (0.5 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going. To make it slower: insert 2 observers: one more column in the 2-wide ring; each step adds 4 ticks (0.2 s) and 2 blocks, up to every 198 ticks (9.9 s).

made of note block, observer · on for 2 of 10 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 32 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 10 ticks (0.5 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
