Clock

every 74 ticks (3.7 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 74 ticks (3.7 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, redstone block, sticky piston · 1 other block choice on its page · on for 37 of 74 ticks
