Notes from its designer

A clock that pulses exactly every 10 minutes, set by the 188 stones in its first hopper, and sealed: no dropped item can reach its hoppers. Two hoppers facing each other pass the stones one per 8 gt; each hopper's comparator powers a sticky piston, and the two pistons hold one redstone block beside the hopper that receives (a locked hopper still receives) until the sender is empty; a stone covers each hopper. A repeater in one piston's path holds the redstone block a few ticks longer, which trims the time to exactly 10 minutes. Copper bulbs (each toggled once per cycle and read by a comparator) double the time per bulb. Catalog parts: comparators, a repeater delay, copper-bulb T flip-flops as dividers.
