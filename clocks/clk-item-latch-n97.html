A longer version of the tested clk-item-latch clock, which pulses every 30 ticks (1.5 s) with 17 blocks; this one is 97 steps longer (n = 97). Each step: one more stone in hopper A (same blocks; up to 5 stacks of 64), which adds 16 ticks (0.8 s) (same blocks). This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 1582 ticks (1 min 19.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Latch hopper clock, item-count tuned: two hoppers facing each other pass N = n + 2 stones from one to the other and back, one per 8 gt; a waxed copper bulb remembers the direction: the comparators reading the hoppers light a torch when their hopper is EMPTY, and those torches (one under the bulb, one under a stone beside it) toggle the bulb when the source runs dry; a comparator reading the bulb lights a dust on the stone above hopper A (locks A) and that stone's wall torch locks hopper B when the bulb is off.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 3168 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 1582 ticks (1 min 19.1 s), on for 1576 ticks (1 min 18.8 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
