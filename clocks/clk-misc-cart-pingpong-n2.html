A longer version of the tested clk-misc-cart-pingpong clock, which pulses every 37 ticks (1.85 s) with 16 blocks; this one is 2 steps longer (n = 2). Each step: one more powered rail (on a stone support) in the east stretch, which adds 5 ticks (0.25 s) and 2 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 47 ticks (2.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Notes from its designer

P = 37 + 5n gt, 16 + 2n = 16 pieces (blocks, 1 lever and the cart). An empty minecart bounces between two stone bumpers on a straight track: the powered rail at each end launches the resting cart away from the bumper behind it and speeds it up; it coasts over the plain rails, is sped up by the east stretch of powered rails, stops against the far bumper and is launched back. A switched-on lever on a support powers the east stretch; the detector rail next to the west end powers the west launcher: it turns on as the cart passes and off at its next 20 gt check once the cart has left. The cart starts on the east launcher. Every bounce clamps the cart against a bumper, so from the second lap its motion repeats exactly.
