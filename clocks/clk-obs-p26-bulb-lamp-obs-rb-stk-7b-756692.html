every 13 ticks (0.65 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 13 ticks (0.65 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

made of copper bulb, observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · also works with note block · on for 2 of 13 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 13 ticks (0.65 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: P26: 1 copper bulb, 2 observers, 1 redstone block, 1 redstone lamp, 2 sticky pistons; changing in place: copper_bulb, observer, redstone_block, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 40 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 1 tick (0.05 s); then it repeats every 13 ticks (0.65 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
