Clock

every 13 ticks (0.65 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 13 ticks (0.65 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of copper bulb, observer, piston, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 2 of 13 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 13 ticks (0.65 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: P26: 1 copper bulb, 3 observers, 1 piston, 1 redstone lamp, 1 sticky piston; changing in place: copper_bulb, observer, piston, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 43 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 13 ticks (0.65 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
