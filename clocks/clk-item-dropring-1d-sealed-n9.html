A longer version of the 17-block dropper ring, sealed clock that pulses every 27 ticks (1.35 s): 9 steps longer. Each step: make the dropper ring one column longer (2 more hoppers); sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 14 ticks (0.7 s) and 4 blocks.

Every clock that pulses every 153 ticks (7.65 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: The sealed twin of clk-item-dropring-1d: each member is clk-item-dropring-1d's with glass on its open hopper tops, so no dropped item can change its timing (clk-misc's audit: a dropped item changes or breaks the open family); period 27 + 14n, 17 + 4n blocks.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 461 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 153 ticks (7.65 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
