Every clock that pulses every 9 ticks (0.45 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: ODD period 9: a ring of 3 delay-2 + 3 delay-1 repeaters + dust (loop delay 18 gt) holds TWO identical 4 gt pulses 9 gt apart: one loaded at placement (a powered repeater), the other started by a 1 gt redstone block next to the corner dust at tick 5 (a second parity class).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 40 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 9 ticks (0.45 s), on for 4 ticks (0.2 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
