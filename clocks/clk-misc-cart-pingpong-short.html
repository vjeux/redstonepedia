Notes from its designer

Minecart ping-pong, short. An empty minecart bounces between two stone bumpers on a straight track: the powered rail at each end launches the resting cart away from the bumper behind it and speeds it up; it coasts over the plain rails, is sped up by the east stretch of powered rails, stops against the far bumper and is launched back. A switched-on lever on a support powers the east stretch; the detector rail next to the west end powers the west launcher: it turns on as the cart passes and off at its next 20 gt check once the cart has left. The cart starts on the east launcher. Every bounce clamps the cart against a bumper, so from the second lap its motion repeats exactly.
