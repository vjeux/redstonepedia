Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 3 ticks (0.15 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse.

made of comparator, hopper · on for 5 of 8 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Digital P=8 hopper pair: the analog pair (3 swords, cooldowns 5 apart: the east-facing hopper A holds 1 or 2 swords, strength 3 or 6) read by a SUBTRACT comparator whose side input is a constant 3 from a second comparator reading a dead-end hopper holding one sword (facing down into air, it never moves): output max(s(A) - 3, 0) = 0 or 3, digital, P = 8.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 28 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 5 ticks (0.25 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
