Every clock that pulses every 10,816 ticks (9 min 0.8 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Divider chain: a base comparator FADING ring (6 + 6 compare comparators keep a strength that drops one level per lap; a wall torch refills the empty ring) and 5 master-slave T flip-flop stage(s) (two latch repeaters locked alternately by repeaters reading the previous torch and its block; the slave's dust bends into a target block holding a torch whose output runs back to the master): period = the ring's x 2^5

Its output over time

Not simulated: its output stayed off for the first 4000 ticks in the browser's simulation.
