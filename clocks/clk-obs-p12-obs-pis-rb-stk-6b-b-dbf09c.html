Clock

every 12 ticks (0.6 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 12 ticks (0.6 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 6 of 12 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: P12: 3 observers, 1 piston, 1 redstone block, 1 sticky piston; changing in place: observer, piston, redstone_block, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 47 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 11 ticks (0.55 s); then it repeats every 12 ticks (0.6 s), on for 6 ticks (0.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
