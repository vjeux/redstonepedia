A longer version of the tested clk-obs-obs-ring clock, which pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s) with 4 blocks; this one is 48 steps longer (n = 48). Each step: insert 2 observers: one more column in the 2-wide ring (each observer watches the previous one), which adds 4 ticks (0.2 s) and 2 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 200 ticks (10 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 604 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 200 ticks (10 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
