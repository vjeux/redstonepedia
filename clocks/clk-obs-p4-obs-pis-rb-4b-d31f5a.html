Clock

every 4 ticks (0.2 s) · 4 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 4 ticks (0.2 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 2 ticks (0.1 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer · on for 2 of 4 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 18 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 4 ticks (0.2 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
