Every clock that pulses every 19 ticks (0.95 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A 19-tick clock a builder can wire, as small as the 7-block record: an observer tap watching the build gives one pulse per cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 73 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 16 ticks (0.8 s); then it repeats every 19 ticks (0.95 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
