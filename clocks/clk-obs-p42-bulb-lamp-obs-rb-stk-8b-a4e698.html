Clock

every 21 ticks (1.05 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 21 ticks (1.05 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), its period changes to 28 ticks (1.4 s) (in 4 of 8 of our test builds). Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · also works with note block · on for 16 of 21 ticks
