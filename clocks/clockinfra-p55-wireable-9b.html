Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A 55-tick clock a builder can wire, as small as the 9-block record: a calibrated sculk sensor loop with an observer and a sticky piston; a comparator reads it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 176 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 11 ticks (0.55 s); then it repeats every 55 ticks (2.75 s), on for 28 ticks (1.4 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
