Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 9 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Stops for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back): start it again the way you started it after building it.

made of copper bulb, note block, observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 3 of 8 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its comparator (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it reads the clock and passes on every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 37 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 3 ticks (0.15 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
