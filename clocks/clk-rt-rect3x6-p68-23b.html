Every clock that pulses every 68 ticks (3.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Rectangle ring: the 3x6 perimeter clockwise from the north-west corner = [b, 4*, b, 4, 4, b, 4, b, 4, b, 4, 4, 4, 2] (number = repeater delay, * = powered at placement, b = block, d = dust, T = wall torch on the block before it, c = comparator); 0 torches: a pulse ring of loop delay 68 gt

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 204 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 68 ticks (3.4 s), on for 8 ticks (0.4 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
