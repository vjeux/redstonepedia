Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 645 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 84 ticks (4.2 s); then it repeats every 187 ticks (9.35 s), on for 20 ticks (1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
