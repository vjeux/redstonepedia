Clock

every 5 ticks (0.25 s) · 5 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 5 ticks (0.25 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 3 ticks (0.15 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of oak leaves, observer, sticky piston · 1 other block choice on its page · on for 2 of 5 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 5 ticks (0.25 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 18 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 3 ticks (0.15 s); then it repeats every 5 ticks (0.25 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
