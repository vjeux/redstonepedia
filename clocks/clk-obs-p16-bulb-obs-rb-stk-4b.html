Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 4 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. Stops for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back): start it again the way you started it after building it. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, observer, redstone block, sticky piston · also works with trapdoor, note block, piston · on for 4 of 8 ticks
