Clock

every 24 ticks (1.2 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 24 ticks (1.2 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: there is no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 12 of 24 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 24 ticks (1.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: changing in place: observer, redstone_block, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 74 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 24 ticks (1.2 s), on for 12 ticks (0.6 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
