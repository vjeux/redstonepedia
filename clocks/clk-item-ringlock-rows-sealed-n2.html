Comes loaded with 5 stone in its containers: the download includes them; building it by hand, put them in first.

A longer version of the tested ring lock, sealed clock, which pulses every 76 ticks (3.8 s) with 24 blocks; this one is 2 steps longer (n = 2). Each step: lengthen the 3-wide ring by one row (2 hoppers), still 5 stones; sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 14 ticks (0.7 s) and 4 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 104 ticks (5.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: The sealed twin of clk-item-ringlock-rows: each member is clk-item-ringlock-rows's with glass on its open hopper tops, so no dropped item can change its timing (clk-misc's audit: a dropped item changes or breaks the open family); period 76 + 14n, 24 + 4n blocks.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 367 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 55 ticks (2.75 s); then it repeats every 104 ticks (5.2 s), on for 39 ticks (1.95 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
