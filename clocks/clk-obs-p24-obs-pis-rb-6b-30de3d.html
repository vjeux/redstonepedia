Clock

every 12 ticks (0.6 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 12 ticks (0.6 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone block · on for 7 of 12 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 12 ticks (0.6 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: P24: 1 copper bulb, 2 observers, 2 pistons, 1 redstone block; changing in place: observer, piston, redstone_block (copper bulb only moved or static).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 49 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 12 ticks (0.6 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
