A longer version of the tested observer ring with a relay block clock, which pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s) with 7 blocks; this one is 42 steps longer (n = 42). Each step: insert 2 observers: one more column in the 2-wide ring. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 178 ticks (8.9 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 536 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 178 ticks (8.9 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
