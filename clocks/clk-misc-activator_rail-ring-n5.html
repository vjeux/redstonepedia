Clock

every 30 ticks (1.5 s) · 16 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 30 ticks (1.5 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

made of activator rail, observer · also works with copper bulb, crafter, door, trapdoor, note block, fence gate, powered rail, mob head · on for 2 of 30 ticks

A longer version of the tested observer ring with a relay block clock, which pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s) with 7 blocks; this one is 5 steps longer (n = 5). Each step: insert 2 observers: the 2-wide ring one row higher. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 30 ticks (1.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 92 ticks, as the 3D view plays it (the design names no output, so this is its observer that turns on most often): the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 30 ticks (1.5 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.

name: clk-misc-activator_rail-ring-n5
# Extendable family clk-misc-activator_rail-ring (n = 5): P = 10 + 4n gt = 30 gt, 6 + 2n = 16 blocks. Relay: an activator rail standing on the observer that powers it: `powered` follows its power at once.
# It sits at the top of a 2-wide ring of 15 observers (each watches the one before it, 2 gt per observer); the observer under it
# powers it and carries it, the next one watches it. The relay adds no delay but takes a lattice cell, so an odd number of
# observers closes the ring. Extension: insert 2 observers (the ring one row higher).
# Generator: mctas clock misc ring --relay activator_rail --n N --out FILE.
