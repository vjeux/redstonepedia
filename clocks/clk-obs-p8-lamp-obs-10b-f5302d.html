Every clock that pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A ring of 9 observers and one lamp relay (10 cells): the observer before the relay powers it, the next one watches it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 32 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 8 ticks (0.4 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
