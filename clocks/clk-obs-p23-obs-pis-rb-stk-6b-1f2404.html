Clock

every 23 ticks (1.15 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 23 ticks (1.15 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: there is no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 18 of 23 ticks

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 75 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 23 ticks (1.15 s), on for 18 ticks (0.9 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
