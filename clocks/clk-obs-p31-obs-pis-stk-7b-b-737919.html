Clock

every 31 ticks (1.55 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 31 ticks (1.55 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, sticky piston · on for 2 of 31 ticks

The output (orange) over the first 93 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 31 ticks (1.55 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
