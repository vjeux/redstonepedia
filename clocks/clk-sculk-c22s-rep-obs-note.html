every 22 ticks (1.1 s) · 5 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 22 ticks (1.1 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it.

Every clock that pulses every 22 ticks (1.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Self-starting: R is placed powered with no input; its turn-off is the first observer pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 76 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 10 ticks (0.5 s); then it repeats every 22 ticks (1.1 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
