A longer version of the tested clk-obs-odd-a clock, which pulses every 11 ticks (0.55 s) with 9 blocks; this one is 39 steps longer (n = 39). Each step: raise the observer tower by one: one more observer in each column. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 167 ticks (8.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 503 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 167 ticks (8.35 s), on for 161 ticks (8.05 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.

name: clk-obs-odd-a-n39
# Extendable family odd-a (n = 39): P = 11 + 4n gt, 8 + 2n blocks. the odd piston stage: observer O1 quasi-powers a sticky piston and O2 (watching O1) keeps it powered from above for 4 gt, so the redstone block it pushes LANDS under a normal piston that fires the next tick (+1 gt): the observer watching that piston's head fires 5 gt after O1; the tower carries the pulse back, 2 gt per observer. Extension: raise the observer tower by one: one more observer in each column. Generator: mctas clock obs family odd-a --n N --out FILE.
