Clock

every 6 ticks (0.3 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 6 ticks (0.3 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going (in 2 of 8 of our test builds). Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, note block, observer, sticky piston · on for 2 of 6 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 24 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 6 ticks (0.3 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
