Every clock that pulses every 167 ticks (8.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 615 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 114 ticks (5.7 s); then it repeats every 167 ticks (8.35 s), on for 20 ticks (1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water where the view shows it. Build it facing the way the download does, without turning it: turned another way it stops or changes its timing.
