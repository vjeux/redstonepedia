Clock

every 7 ticks (0.35 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 7 ticks (0.35 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of note block, observer, sticky piston · on for 2 of 7 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 7 ticks (0.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: P7: 2 note blocks, 2 observers, 2 sticky pistons; changing in place: note_block, observer, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 29 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 8 ticks (0.4 s); then it repeats every 7 ticks (0.35 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
