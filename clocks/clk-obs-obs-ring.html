Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 4 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going. To make it slower: insert 2 observers: one more column in the 2-wide ring (each observer watches the previous one); each step adds 4 ticks (0.2 s) and 2 blocks, up to every 600 ticks (30 s).

every 8 ticksClockpicture from the real game; the live 3D view replaces it when available

Every clock that pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 28 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
