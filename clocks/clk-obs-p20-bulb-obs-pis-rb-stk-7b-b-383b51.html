Clock

every 10 ticks (0.5 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 10 ticks (0.5 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 6 of 10 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 33 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 3 ticks (0.15 s); then it repeats every 10 ticks (0.5 s), on for 6 ticks (0.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
