Every clock that pulses every 7 ticks (0.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 35 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 14 ticks (0.7 s); then it repeats every 7 ticks (0.35 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
