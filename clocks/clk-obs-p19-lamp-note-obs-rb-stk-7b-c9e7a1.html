Clock

every 19 ticks (0.95 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 19 ticks (0.95 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 9 of 19 ticks
