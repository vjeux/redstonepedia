Clock

every 13 ticks (0.65 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 13 ticks (0.65 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · also works with note block, stone · on for 8 of 13 ticks
