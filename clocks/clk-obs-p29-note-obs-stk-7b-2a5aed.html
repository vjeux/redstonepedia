Clock

every 29 ticks (1.45 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 29 ticks (1.45 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, sticky piston · on for 1 of 29 ticks

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 108 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 21 ticks (1.05 s); then it repeats every 29 ticks (1.45 s), on for 1 tick (0.05 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
