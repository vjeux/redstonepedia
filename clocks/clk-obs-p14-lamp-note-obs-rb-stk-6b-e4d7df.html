Clock

every 14 ticks (0.7 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 14 ticks (0.7 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 10 of 14 ticks
