Clock

every 44 ticks (2.2 s) · 3 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 44 ticks (2.2 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Sounds near its sculk sensor (footsteps, another machine) add pulses: keep noise away from it.

made of oak trapdoor, observer, sculk sensor · also works with trapdoor, note block, fence gate, piston · on for 30 of 44 ticks

every 44 ticksClockpicture from the real game; the live 3D view replaces it when available

Every clock that pulses every 44 ticks (2.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 132 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 44 ticks (2.2 s), on for 30 ticks (1.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
