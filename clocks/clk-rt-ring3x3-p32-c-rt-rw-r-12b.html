Every clock that pulses every 32 ticks (1.6 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Rectangle ring: the 3x3 perimeter clockwise from the north-west corner = [b, 2*, b, 4*, b, T, d, c] (number = repeater delay, * = powered at placement, b = block, d = dust, T = wall torch on the block before it, c = comparator); 1 torch(es): an inverter loop, period 2 x 16 gt

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 102 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 32 ticks (1.6 s), on for 16 ticks (0.8 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
