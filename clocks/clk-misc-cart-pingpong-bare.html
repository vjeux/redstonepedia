Details: how it works, measurements and properties

Notes from its designer

Minecart ping-pong, bare. An empty minecart bounces between two stone bumpers on a straight track: the powered rail at each end launches the resting cart away from the bumper behind it and speeds it up; it coasts over the plain rails, is sped up by the east stretch of powered rails, stops against the far bumper and is launched back. A switched-on lever on a support powers the east stretch. The cart starts on the east launcher. Every bounce clamps the cart against a bumper, so from the second lap its motion repeats exactly.
