Every clock that pulses every 14 ticks (0.7 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: P14: 1 note block, 2 observers, 1 redstone lamp, 1 sticky piston, 1 stone; changing in place: note_block, observer, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston (stone only moved or static).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 44 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 14 ticks (0.7 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
