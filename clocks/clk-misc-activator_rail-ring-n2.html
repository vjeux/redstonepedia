Clock

every 18 ticks (0.9 s) · 10 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 18 ticks (0.9 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

made of activator rail, observer · also works with copper bulb, crafter, door, trapdoor, note block, fence gate, powered rail, mob head · on for 2 of 18 ticks

A longer version of the 7-block observer ring with a relay block clock that pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s): 2 steps longer. Each step: insert 2 observers: the 2-wide ring one row higher.

Every clock that pulses every 18 ticks (0.9 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 56 ticks, as the 3D view plays it (the design names no output, so this is its observer that turns on most often): the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 18 ticks (0.9 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
