A longer version of the tested clk-rt-counter clock, which pulses every 1596 ticks (1 min 19.8 s) with 44 blocks; this one is 2 steps longer (n = 3). Each step: wrap one more counter level around the clock (a lossless 2 + 2 comparator ring with one subtract comparator, its pulse shaper and refill torch: 26 blocks): the period x 14, which adds 155610 ticks (2 h 9 min) and 26 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 312816 ticks (4 h 20 min): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: EXTENDABLE comparator counters, n = 1 levels over a fading ring (P114): P = 114 x 14^n; each level a lossless 2 + 2 comparator ring decremented by one level per pulse of the level inside it and refilled by its own torch (26 blocks)

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 4000 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 12 ticks (0.6 s); then it repeats every 312816 ticks (4 h 20 min), on for 312814 ticks (4 h 20 min) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
