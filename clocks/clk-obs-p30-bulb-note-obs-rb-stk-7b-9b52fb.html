Clock

every 15 ticks (0.75 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 15 ticks (0.75 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of copper bulb, note block, observer, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 6 of 15 ticks
