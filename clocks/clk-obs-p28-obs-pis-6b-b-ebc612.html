Clock

every 4 ticks (0.2 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 4 ticks (0.2 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 2 ticks (0.1 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), its period changes to 4 ticks (0.2 s).

made of observer, piston · on for 2 of 4 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: P28: 4 observers, 2 pistons; changing in place: observer, piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 22 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 10 ticks (0.5 s); then it repeats every 4 ticks (0.2 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
