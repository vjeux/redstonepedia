Every clock that pulses every 25 ticks (1.25 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A calibrated sculk sensor drives repeater(s) of delay [4], then wall torch on a stone, dispenser beside the torch, 1 from the sensor (travel time)

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 75 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 25 ticks (1.25 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
