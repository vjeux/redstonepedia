Clock

every 7 ticks (0.35 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 7 ticks (0.35 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 2 of 7 ticks
