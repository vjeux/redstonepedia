Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going. Hard to wire into a circuit: there is no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone block · on for 2 of 8 ticks

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 39 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 15 ticks (0.75 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
