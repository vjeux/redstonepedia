Clock

every 18 ticks (0.9 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 18 ticks (0.9 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, piston, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 10 of 18 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 66 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 12 ticks (0.6 s); then it repeats every 18 ticks (0.9 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
