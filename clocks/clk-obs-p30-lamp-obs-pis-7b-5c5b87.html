Clock

every 6 ticks (0.3 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 6 ticks (0.3 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse.

made of observer, piston, redstone lamp · also works with note block, sticky piston · on for 2 of 6 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 26 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 8 ticks (0.4 s); then it repeats every 6 ticks (0.3 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
