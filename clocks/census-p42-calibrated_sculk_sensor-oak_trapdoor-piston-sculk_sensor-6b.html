Clock

every 42 ticks (2.1 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 42 ticks (2.1 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it. If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of calibrated sculk sensor, oak trapdoor, piston, sculk sensor · also works with dispenser, note block, sticky piston · on for 30 of 42 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 150 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 24 ticks (1.2 s); then it repeats every 42 ticks (2.1 s), on for 30 ticks (1.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
