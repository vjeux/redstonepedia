A longer version of the tested hopper ring, sealed clock, which pulses every 14 ticks (0.7 s) with 12 blocks; this one is 28 steps longer (n = 29). Each step: lengthen the 2-wide hopper ring by one column (2 hoppers); the two items stay half a ring apart; sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 7 ticks (0.35 s) and 4 blocks. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 210 ticks (10.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: The sealed twin of clk-item-hopper-ring-2: each member is clk-item-hopper-ring-2's with glass on its open hopper tops, so no dropped item can change its timing (clk-misc's audit: a dropped item changes or breaks the open family); period 7 + 7n, 8 + 4n blocks.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 427 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 7 ticks (0.35 s); then it repeats every 210 ticks (10.5 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
