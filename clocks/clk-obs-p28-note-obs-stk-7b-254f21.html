Clock

every 28 ticks (1.4 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 28 ticks (1.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: there is no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, sticky piston · on for 1 of 28 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 28 ticks (1.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 130 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 46 ticks (2.3 s); then it repeats every 28 ticks (1.4 s), on for 1 tick (0.05 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
