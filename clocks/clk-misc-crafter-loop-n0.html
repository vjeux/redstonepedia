Notes from its designer

P = 91 + 28n = 91 gt, 7 + 2n = 7 blocks. A crafter crafts back and forth between 1 slime block and 9 slime balls; 1 hopper(s) carry each result back into it (one item per 8 gt). A comparator reads the hopper that feeds the crafter through a stone and two observers pulse the crafter on both of its edges: when that hopper empties the crafter holds a whole recipe and crafts 4 gt later; when it fills, the crafter is empty or partly filled and nothing matches. Extension: 2 more hoppers (+28 gt: the block and the 9 balls each cross 2 more hoppers).
