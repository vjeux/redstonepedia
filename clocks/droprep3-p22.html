Every clock that pulses every 22 ticks (1.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its comparator (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it reads the clock and passes on every pulse.

How this design does it: Dropper ring with a delayed fire and three filled cells (clock item sweep-dropring --triples --delays): a hopper ring 5 deep with two droppers in the bottom row; the comparator in the inner column reads the top-row hopper above it and fires the droppers through two repeaters (delays 1 and 4, on stone).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 69 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 3 ticks (0.15 s); then it repeats every 22 ticks (1.1 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
