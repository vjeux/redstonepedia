Clock

every 10 ticks (0.5 s) · 10 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 10 ticks (0.5 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 4 ticks (0.2 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse. It can stop for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back): start it again the way you started it after building it.

made of copper bulb, note block, observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · also works with redstone lamp · on for 6 of 10 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its comparator (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it reads the clock and passes on every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 47 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 17 ticks (0.85 s); then it repeats every 10 ticks (0.5 s), on for 6 ticks (0.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
