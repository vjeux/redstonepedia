Clock

every 21 ticks (1.05 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 21 ticks (1.05 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Stops for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back): start it again the way you started it after building it. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, piston, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 12 of 21 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 69 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 21 ticks (1.05 s), on for 12 ticks (0.6 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
