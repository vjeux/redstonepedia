Clock

every 131 ticks (6.55 s) · 39 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 131 ticks (6.55 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it.

every 131 ticksClock3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

A longer version of the tested clk-sculk-hairpin (one block swapped: 2 versions) clock, which pulses every 35 ticks (1.75 s) with 15 blocks; this one is 6 steps longer (n = 8). Each step: one more delay-4 repeater on a stone in each arm of the hairpin (the dust U-turn moves one block out), which adds 16 ticks (0.8 s) and 4 blocks. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 131 ticks (6.55 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 535 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 142 ticks (7.1 s); then it repeats every 131 ticks (6.55 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
