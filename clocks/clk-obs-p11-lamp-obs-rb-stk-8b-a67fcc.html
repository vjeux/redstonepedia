Clock

every 11 ticks (0.55 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 11 ticks (0.55 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), its period changes to 6 ticks (0.3 s) (in 4 of 8 of our test builds).

made of observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 1 of 11 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 43 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 10 ticks (0.5 s); then it repeats every 11 ticks (0.55 s), on for 1 tick (0.05 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
