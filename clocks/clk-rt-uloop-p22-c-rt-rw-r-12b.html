Every clock that pulses every 22 ticks (1.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: U-loop: a wall torch on block A -> top row [delay-4 repeater (powered at placement), delay-4 repeater] -> block F -> a wall torch on F -> bottom row [comparator, dust] -> A; 2 torches = a pulse ring of loop delay 22 gt

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 66 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: it repeats every 22 ticks (1.1 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
