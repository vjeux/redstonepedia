Clock

every 4 ticks (0.2 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 4 ticks (0.2 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 2 ticks (0.1 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse.

Every clock that pulses every 4 ticks (0.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A big dripleaf clock: an armor stand rests on a big dripleaf; the dripleaf tilts under it (none -> unstable); an observer watching the leaf pulses a redstone dust that steps down and powers the dirt under the leaf, and power resets the tilt (and blocks re-tilting until it ends); then the stand tilts it again.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 22 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 10 ticks (0.5 s); then it repeats every 4 ticks (0.2 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
