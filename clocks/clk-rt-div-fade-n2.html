A longer version of the tested clk-rt-div-fade clock, which pulses every 228 ticks (11.4 s) with 34 blocks; this one is 1 step longer (n = 2). Each step: append one master-slave T flip-flop stage (18 blocks) to the chain on the P114 comparator fading ring: the period doubles, which adds 228 ticks (11.4 s) and 18 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 456 ticks (22.8 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 916 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 456 ticks (22.8 s), on for 342 ticks (17.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
