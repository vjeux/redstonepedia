Clock

every 12 ticks (0.6 s) · 5 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 12 ticks (0.6 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, piston, sticky piston · on for 2 of 12 ticks

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: P12: 1 note block, 2 observers, 1 piston, 1 sticky piston; changing in place: note_block, observer, piston, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 38 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 12 ticks (0.6 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.

Notes from its designer

P12: 1 note block, 2 observers, 1 piston, 1 sticky piston; changing in place: note_block, observer, piston, sticky_piston. Found by the clk-obs exhaustive census of every 5-block structure (all orientations, every block placed last as a kick); minimal: no single block can be removed keeping the period. Output: the changing observer with the most rising edges (declared, so every rotation reads the same cell).
