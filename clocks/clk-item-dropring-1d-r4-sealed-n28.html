A longer version of the 23-block dropper ring, sealed clock that pulses every 42 ticks (2.1 s): 28 steps longer. Each step: make the dropper ring one column longer (2 more hoppers); sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 14 ticks (0.7 s) and 4 blocks.

Every clock that pulses every 434 ticks (21.7 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 870 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 434 ticks (21.7 s), on for 8 ticks (0.4 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
