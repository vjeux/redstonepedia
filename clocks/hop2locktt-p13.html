Every clock that pulses every 13 ticks (0.65 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Hopper pair with a double-torch lock (fill k=5): 2 swords; the comparator reading the east-facing hopper A powers stone S; a torch standing on S lights the dust on the next stone, whose wall torch points into the west-facing hopper B: two inversions (2 + 2 gt) after the comparator, B is locked while A holds a sword.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 41 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 13 ticks (0.65 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
