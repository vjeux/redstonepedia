Clock

every 22 ticks (1.1 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 22 ticks (1.1 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Sounds near its sculk sensor (footsteps, another machine) add pulses: keep noise away from it.

made of calibrated sculk sensor, dispenser, observer, sticky piston · on for 10 of 22 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 22 ticks (1.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its comparator (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it reads the clock and passes on every pulse.

How this design does it: A calibrated sculk sensor watched by a chain of 2 observer(s) (each watching the previous); an empty dispenser at the last output, 2 from the sensor (travel time): the observers' pulse after the sensor re-arms is heard; plus a sticky piston beside the chain (moves parts of it: the hybrid)

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 79 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 22 ticks (1.1 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
