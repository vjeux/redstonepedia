Its output pulses by itself every 47 ticks (2.35 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Sounds near its sculk sensor (footsteps, another machine) add pulses: keep noise away from it.

Every clock that pulses every 47 ticks (2.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A plain sculk sensor watched by a chain of 4 observer(s) (each watching the previous); an empty dispenser at the last output, 5 from the sensor (travel time): the observers' pulse after the sensor re-arms is heard

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 153 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 12 ticks (0.6 s); then it repeats every 47 ticks (2.35 s), on for 30 ticks (1.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
