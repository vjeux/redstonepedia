Clock

every 7 ticks (0.35 s) · 8 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 7 ticks (0.35 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of copper bulb, observer, piston, redstone lamp, sticky piston · also works with note block · on for 2 of 7 ticks

every 7 ticksClockpicture from the real game; the live 3D view replaces it when available

Every clock that pulses every 7 ticks (0.35 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: changing in place: copper_bulb, observer, piston, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 39 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 18 ticks (0.9 s); then it repeats every 7 ticks (0.35 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
