Clock

every 14 ticks (0.7 s) · 9 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 14 ticks (0.7 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of copper bulb, note block, observer, piston, redstone lamp, sticky piston · on for 2 of 14 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 14 ticks (0.7 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 55 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 14 ticks (0.7 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
