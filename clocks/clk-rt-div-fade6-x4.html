Every clock that pulses every 5408 ticks (4 min 30.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Divider chain: a base comparator FADING ring (6 + 6 compare comparators keep a strength that drops one level per lap; a wall torch refills the empty ring) and 4 master-slave T flip-flop stage(s) (two latch repeaters locked alternately by repeaters reading the previous torch and its block; the slave's dust bends into a target block holding a torch whose output runs back to the master): period = the ring's x 2^4

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 4000 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 20 ticks (1 s); then it repeats every 5408 ticks (4 min 30.4 s), on for 2704 ticks (2 min 15.2 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
