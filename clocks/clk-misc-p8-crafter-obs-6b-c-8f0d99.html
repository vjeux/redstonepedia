Clock

every 8 ticks (0.4 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 8 ticks (0.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

made of crafter, observer · also works with comparator, copper bulb, note block, piston, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston, stone · on for 2 of 8 ticks

every 8 ticksClockpicture from the real game; the live 3D view replaces it when available

Every clock that pulses every 8 ticks (0.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 30 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 8 ticks (0.4 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
