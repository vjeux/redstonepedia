every 43 ticks (2.15 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 43 ticks (2.15 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it.

Every clock that pulses every 43 ticks (2.15 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A plain sculk sensor watched by a chain of 4 observer(s) (each watching the previous); an iron trapdoor at the last output, 3 from the sensor (travel time): the observers' pulse after the sensor re-arms is heard

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 142 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 43 ticks (2.15 s), on for 30 ticks (1.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
