Clock

every 72 ticks (3.6 s) · 19 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 72 ticks (3.6 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

Uses a tripwire: anything that crosses the string (a mob, a player, a dropped item) adds a pulse, so enclose it. A lamp on its output stays lit: the output is off for only 2 ticks (0.1 s) at a time, and a redstone lamp goes dark only after more than 4 ticks (0.2 s) without power. Redstone dust there follows every pulse.

made of oak boat, tripwire, tripwire hook · on for 70 of 72 ticks

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 217 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 1 tick (0.05 s); then it repeats every 72 ticks (3.6 s), on for 70 ticks (3.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
