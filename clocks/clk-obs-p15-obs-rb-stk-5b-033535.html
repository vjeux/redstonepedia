Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 47 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 15 ticks (0.75 s), on for 8 ticks (0.4 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
