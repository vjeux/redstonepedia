A longer version of the tested hopper ring, sealed clock, which pulses every 14 ticks (0.7 s) with 8 blocks; this one is 1 step longer (n = 1). Each step: lengthen the 2-wide hopper ring by one column (2 hoppers); sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 14 ticks (0.7 s) and 4 blocks. Tested in the real game too.

Every clock that pulses every 28 ticks (1.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: The sealed twin of clk-item-hopper-ring: each member is clk-item-hopper-ring's with glass on its open hopper tops, so no dropped item can change its timing (clk-misc's audit: a dropped item changes or breaks the open family); period 14 + 14n, 8 + 4n blocks.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 91 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 7 ticks (0.35 s); then it repeats every 28 ticks (1.4 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
