Every clock that pulses every 48 ticks (2.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A plain sculk sensor watched by a chain of 2 observer(s) (each watching the previous); the last observer powers a stone and an oak fence gate beside the stone, 4 from the sensor (travel time): the observers' pulse after the sensor re-arms is heard

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 146 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 48 ticks (2.4 s), on for 30 ticks (1.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
