Clock

every 26 ticks (1.3 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 26 ticks (1.3 s), forever. It starts on its own once built. To make it slower: one more delay-4 repeater on a stone at the end of the line (the observer and dispenser move one block out): +8 gt of delay and +1 gt of travel; each step adds 9 ticks (0.45 s) and 2 blocks, up to every 134 ticks (6.7 s).

Uses a sculk sensor: other sounds nearby (footsteps, another machine) can add pulses, so keep noise away from it.

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 108 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 30 ticks (1.5 s); then it repeats every 26 ticks (1.3 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
