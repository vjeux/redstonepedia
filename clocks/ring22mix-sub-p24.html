Every clock that pulses every 24 ticks (1.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Digital P=24, 2x2 hopper ring with mixed items: 4 hoppers in a ring holding 2 swords and 2 stones (in another arrangement than the P=16 one); the reader is a SUBTRACT comparator whose side input is a constant 1 (a second comparator reading a dead-end hopper holding one stone): output max(s - 1, 0) = 2 (a sword passes) or 0 (a stone), digital, P = 24.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 87 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 15 ticks (0.75 s); then it repeats every 24 ticks (1.2 s), on for 7 ticks (0.35 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
