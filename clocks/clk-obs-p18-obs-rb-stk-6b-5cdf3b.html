Clock

every 18 ticks (0.9 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 18 ticks (0.9 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going (in 4 of 8 of our test builds). Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of observer, redstone block, sticky piston · on for 6 of 18 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 56 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 18 ticks (0.9 s), on for 6 ticks (0.3 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
