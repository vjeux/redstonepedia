Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A 90-tick clock a builder can wire, one pulse per cycle: clk-item's hopper ring lock ringlock35-p90 with a usable tap.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 304 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 34 ticks (1.7 s); then it repeats every 90 ticks (4.5 s), on for 39 ticks (1.95 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
