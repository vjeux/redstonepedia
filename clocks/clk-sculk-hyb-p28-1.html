Clock

every 28 ticks (1.4 s) · 5 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 28 ticks (1.4 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Sounds near its sculk sensor (footsteps, another machine) add pulses: keep noise away from it.

made of calibrated sculk sensor, note block, observer · on for 10 of 28 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 28 ticks (1.4 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: A calibrated sculk sensor watched by a chain of 2 observer(s) (each watching the previous); the last observer powers a stone and a note block beside the stone, 4 from the sensor (travel time): the observers' pulse after the sensor re-arms is heard

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 86 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 28 ticks (1.4 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
