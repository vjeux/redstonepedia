Clock

every 21 ticks (1.05 s) · 5 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 21 ticks (1.05 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Sounds near its sculk sensor (footsteps, another machine) add pulses: keep noise away from it.

made of calibrated sculk sensor, dispenser, observer · also works with comparator, note block, sticky piston, stone · on for 10 of 21 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 21 ticks (1.05 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view to start it; then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Calibrated sensor, 3 observers curling back (each watches the previous; the first watches the sensor), empty dispenser at the last output, diagonal to the sensor (travel 1).

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 76 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 21 ticks (1.05 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.

Notes from its designer

Calibrated sensor, 3 observers curling back (each watches the previous; the first watches the sensor), empty dispenser at the last output, diagonal to the sensor (travel 1). The cooldown pulse (A+10) reaches the dispenser at A+16, it fires at A+20, right after the sensor re-arms.
