Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Hopper pair with a delayed self-lock on emptiness (clock item fill k=5, 1 stone): the comparator reading the east-facing hopper A powers stone S; a wall torch on S (lit while A is empty) feeds a repeater pointing into the stone beside A, so A is locked from a repeater delay after it empties until that long after the stone comes back.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 62 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 11 ticks (0.55 s); then it repeats every 17 ticks (0.85 s), on for 10 ticks (0.5 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
