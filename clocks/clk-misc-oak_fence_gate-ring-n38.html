A longer version of the tested observer ring with a relay block clock, which pulses every 10 ticks (0.5 s) with 7 blocks; this one is 38 steps longer (n = 38). Each step: insert 2 observers: the 2-wide ring one row higher. This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 162 ticks (8.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

The view starts it for you, then it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 488 ticks, as the 3D view plays it (the design names no output, so this is its observer that turns on most often): the grey part is the clock starting up, for 2 ticks (0.1 s); then it repeats every 162 ticks (8.1 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
