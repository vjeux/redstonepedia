Clock

every 39 ticks (1.95 s) · 7 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 39 ticks (1.95 s), forever. Built in the game it needs one kick to start (the last block placed); then it keeps going.

Works only facing the way it is shown here: build it from the download without turning it; turned another way it stops or changes its timing (our test tried all 4 directions). Stops for good if its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back) (in 4 of 8 of our test builds): start it again the way you started it after building it. Hard to wire into a circuit: we found no spot beside it where a repeater, a comparator or an observer picks up every pulse.

made of note block, observer, sticky piston · on for 2 of 39 ticks

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 130 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 13 ticks (0.65 s); then it repeats every 39 ticks (1.95 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
