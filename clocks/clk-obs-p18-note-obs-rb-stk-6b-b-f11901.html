Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: P18: 1 note block, 2 observers, 1 redstone block, 2 sticky pistons; changing in place: note_block, observer, redstone_block, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 68 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 14 ticks (0.7 s); then it repeats every 18 ticks (0.9 s), on for 8 ticks (0.4 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
