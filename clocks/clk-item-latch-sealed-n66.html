Comes loaded with 68 stone in its containers: the download includes them; building it by hand, put them in first.

A longer version of the tested clk-item-latch-sealed clock, which pulses every 30 ticks (1.5 s) with 21 blocks; this one is 66 steps longer (n = 66). Each step: one more stone in hopper A (same blocks; up to 5 stacks of 64); sealed: glass on the open hopper tops (no dropped item reaches a hopper), which adds 16 ticks (0.8 s) (same blocks). This length was simulated to exactly its period; the base design was tested in the real game.

Every clock that pulses every 1086 ticks (54.3 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it.

How this design does it: The sealed twin of clk-item-latch: each member is clk-item-latch's with glass on its open hopper tops, so no dropped item can change its timing (clk-misc's audit: a dropped item changes or breaks the open family); period 30 + 16n, 21 blocks.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 2176 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 4 ticks (0.2 s); then it repeats every 1086 ticks (54.3 s), on for 1080 ticks (54 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
