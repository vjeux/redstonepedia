A longer version of the 44-block clock that pulses every 1,596 ticks (1 min 19.8 s): 1 step longer. Each step: wrap one more counter level around the clock (a lossless 2 + 2 comparator ring with one subtract comparator, its pulse shaper and refill torch: 26 blocks): the period x 14, which adds 20,748 ticks (17 min 17.4 s) and 26 blocks.

Every clock that pulses every 22,344 ticks (18 min 37.2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: EXTENDABLE comparator counters, n = 1 levels over a fading ring: P = 114 x 14^n; each level a lossless 2 + 2 comparator ring decremented by one level per pulse of the level inside it and refilled by its own torch (26 blocks)

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 4000 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 12 ticks (0.6 s); then it repeats every 22,344 ticks (18 min 37.2 s), on for 22,342 ticks (18 min 37.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
