Clock

every 12 ticks (0.6 s) · 6 blocks

Its output pulses by itself every 12 ticks (0.6 s), forever. It starts on its own once built.

If its chunk unloads mid-cycle (you walk away and come back), it skips a beat, then keeps going.

made of observer, redstone block, redstone lamp, sticky piston · also works with comparator, copper bulb, note block, piston, stone · on for 2 of 12 ticks

Every clock that pulses every 12 ticks (0.6 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, take the signal from its observer (part of the download, where the view puts its lamp): it watches the clock and pulses every cycle.

How this design does it: changing in place: observer, redstone_block, redstone_lamp, sticky_piston.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 46 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 10 ticks (0.5 s); then it repeats every 12 ticks (0.6 s), on for 2 ticks (0.1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
