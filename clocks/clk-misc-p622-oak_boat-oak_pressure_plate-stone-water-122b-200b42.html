Every clock that pulses every 622 ticks (31.1 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

Press play under the view: it runs by itself: pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 1274 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 30 ticks (1.5 s); then it repeats every 622 ticks (31.1 s), on for 120 ticks (6 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
