Every clock that pulses every 40 ticks (2 s): on the Clocks tab, smallest first (the ones hard to wire into a circuit last).

Try it

It runs by itself: the view plays it; pause and step one tick at a time with the buttons under it. To use it in a circuit, put a repeater where the view puts its lamp: it picks up every pulse.

How this design does it: Divider chain: a base torch + repeater U-loop (delays 2/2: period 20 gt) and 1 master-slave T flip-flop stage(s); each stage = two latch repeaters (master, slave) locked alternately by repeaters reading the previous stage's torch (not S) and block (S), the slave's block holds a torch whose output runs back (dust) to the master: one toggle per period of the previous stage, period 20 x 2^1 = 40 gt

Its output over time

The output (orange) over the first 126 ticks, as the 3D view plays it: the grey part is the clock starting up, for 6 ticks (0.3 s); then it repeats every 40 ticks (2 s), on for 20 ticks (1 s) of them. 20 ticks = 1 second.
